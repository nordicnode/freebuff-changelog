- 2026-09-29 CLI post-click return label for ad clicks and prompts: A new CLI module implements the post-click RETURN label (COD-694), measuring whether a user who clicked a Freebuff ad comes back to the assistant. Because a terminal cannot see focus, the CLI's return signal is the user's next submitted prompt: `away_ms` is recorded as click to next prompt latency, an upper bound on time away, and a second ad click also resolves a pending click as a return. The watcher starts from `recordAdClick` in `cli/src/hooks/use-gravity-ad.ts` and resolves from `routeUserPrompt` in `cli/src/commands/router.ts` via `noteUserTurnForAdClickReturn`, while the shared machine and wire contract live in the new `common/src/ads/click-return.ts` as a clock-injected state machine with a 30-minute watch and no leave grace. (touches: cli/src/ads/click-return.ts, common/src/ads/click-return.ts, cli/src/commands/router.ts, cli/src/hooks/use-gravity-ad.ts) [notable]
