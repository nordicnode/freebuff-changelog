/**
 * ONE AD ON SCREEN AT A TIME: what the chat's two main ad units hold and
 * show, so every other unit can give way.
 *
 * The two main units are the slot above the input (the dock) and the
 * end-of-answer card in the transcript. The rules:
 *
 * 1. A PAIR FROM TWO SUPPLIERS: the ad network always wins.
 *    - network dock + network card in view: the dock goes blank (the card
 *      outranks it);
 *    - network dock + our card: OUR CARD is not drawn
 *      (`suppressOurCards`), and the card request carries
 *      `networkAdInView` so the server answers with the network or nothing;
 *    - our dock + network card in view: our dock goes blank;
 *    - our dock + our card in view: the dock goes blank.
 *    A blank dock requests nothing, pauses its rotation clock and shows the
 *    ad it held when the card leaves the view.
 * 2. Only the newest answer's card is drawn (`keepNewestResponseAd`).
 * 3. Our own units (partner rows, display units) neither show nor request
 *    while an ad-network ad is HELD by the dock (drawn or blank) or DRAWN as
 *    the card (`networkAdOnScreen`). Held and drawn, not "visible right now":
 *    the dock going blank for a card never changes the gate, so a row
 *    appearing cannot move the card and flip the dock back.
 * 4. A sponsored offer held in the dock is never hidden; the card request
 *    for the answer waits until the offer has gone.
 *
 * IN VIEW, WITH HYSTERESIS. The card counts as in view from the first
 * measurement inside the viewport, and stops only after it has been
 * measured outside it (or unmeasurable) for {@link CARD_OUT_OF_VIEW_RELEASE_MS}
 * without a break. A card that cannot be measured never makes the dock go
 * blank, so an unreadable layout cannot leave the dock blank for a session.
 */

/**
 * When a drawn ad must come off the screen, on every client (web workspace and
 * Desktop, CLI, iOS, Android).
 *
 * Without this an ad stays up for as long as nothing replaces it: the web
 * banner asks for its next ad only while the person is active, and inline,
 * phone and partner ads never rotate at all. Two limits, both counted from
 * when this ad was first drawn (a restored ad keeps its first time):
 *
 * - the cap: no ad stays longer than 2 x the rotation interval, active or not;
 * - away: once it has been shown for one rotation interval, it comes off as
 *   soon as the person is away (idle, or the window or app hidden, unfocused
 *   or in the background).
 *
 * The rotation interval is the cadence arm's `rotationMs` from
 * `/api/v1/ads/policy` (3 min on `reduced`, 1 min on `control`); ads that do
 * not rotate, and clients without the policy, use the `reduced` value. The
 * policy route also sends both limits (`adMaxShownMs`, `adAwayExpiryMs`) so a
 * client can read them as they are.
 *
 * Swift (`ios_client/.../AdExpiry.swift`) and Kotlin
 * (`android_client/.../ads/AdExpiry.kt`) restate these numbers.
 */

/**
 * Per-impression engagement accounting for the CLI's ad cards (COD-757): the
 * terminal port of the engagement registry Desktop's retired chat UI used.
 *
 * One `AdEngagement` record per `impUrl`, plus an optional post-click-only
 * record the server merges into it. TRAINING FEATURES ONLY: every value is
 * client-reported, so nothing downstream may bill or convict on it, and it
 * never touches the impression or click reports, which keep their own dedupe.
 *
 * What a terminal can and cannot honestly observe:
 *
 * - Hover is OpenTUI's mouse-over/out on the card. With no mouse reporting
 *   there are simply no hover events.
 * - Visibility. A PINNED card (the dock above the composer, the landing
 *   screen's columns) is laid out outside any scrollbox, so it is on screen
 *   for exactly as long as it is mounted. A card in the TRANSCRIPT is on
 *   screen only while its rows intersect the scrollbox viewport, which its
 *   owner measures and reports; until a measurement succeeds, visibility is
 *   unknown and `visibleAtMs`/`visibleMs` are omitted. There is no 50%
 *   threshold to apply, so `mrc50` is never set.
 * - Focus exists only when the terminal sends DEC 1004 focus reports. Without
 *   them `focusedVisibleMs`, `click.windowFocused` and `postClick` are omitted:
 *   an unknown focus is never reported as "unfocused".
 * - A click has no `isTrusted` and no DOM region; the CTA buttons say `cta`.
 * - Idle and keystrokes (wave 2) come from the CLI's activity tracker and the
 *   composer's keypress count. `idleVisibleMs` needs a known last-input time,
 *   `keysDuringExposure` needs the composer wired; without either, absent.
 *   A keystroke is counted, never identified. `reentries` counts a
 *   transcript card measured back inside the viewport after it left it; a
 *   pinned card has no viewport to leave, so its count is always 0.
 * - Pixel/DOM concepts (`cardWidth`/`cardHeight`, `closestPointer`,
 *   `imageLoadMs`, `copied`) have no terminal equivalent and are never set,
 *   and a CLI ad cannot be dismissed, so `dismissMs` is never set either.
 *
 * Two layers, so the timing rules are tested with a fake clock and no TUI:
 * `createEngagementTracker` (one impression's state machine, possibly drawn
 * in several places at once: the inline pool repeats an ad across slots) and
 * `createEngagementRegistry` (ownership by `impUrl`, the release grace that
 * lets a remount resume the same record, exactly one flush per impression).
 *
 * WHEN A RECORD LEAVES. The final record goes out when the last drawn copy
 * unmounts (after the grace) or when the CLI quits (`closeAll`). Neither is
 * enough on its own in a terminal: a transcript card stays mounted for the
 * whole session, and a quit is not guaranteed. So a live impression also
 * sends CHECKPOINTS -- the record so far, with no `exit` -- which the server
 * merges key by key and the final record later overwrites:
 *
 * - once, the first time a transcript card leaves the viewport after being
 *   seen, or after {@link ENGAGEMENT_CHECKPOINT_MS} of exposure, whichever
 *   comes first;
 * - once, at the first click, because the click is the label the record
 *   exists for.
 *
 * LATE CLICKS. The dock redraws a cached creative under its original
 * `impUrl` long after that impression's record went out, and the server
 * still bills the click. A mount of an already-flushed `impUrl` therefore
 * gets a CLICK-ONLY tracker: it measures nothing about the exposure (that
 * record is closed) and sends only `{ v, impUrl, click }` -- plus `postClick`
 * -- as a merge record. When the impression was already clicked, the first
 * click's features are kept and only the count grows, so a late click never
 * overwrites the click it follows.
 */

/** Drops `undefined` keys so the wire body carries only what is known. */
function defined<T extends Record<string, unknown>>(value: T): T {
  const out: Record<string, unknown> = {}
  for (const [key, entry] of Object.entries(value))
    if (entry !== undefined) out[key] = entry
  return out as T
}

    if (flushed) return false
    for (const owner of owners.values())
      if (owner.placement === 'pinned' || owner.visible === true) return true
    return false
  }
  const isHovering = () => {
    if (flushed) return false
    for (const owner of owners.values()) if (owner.hovering) return true
    return false
  }

  let wasVisible = false
  let wasHovering = false

  /** Accrue the interval since the last event under the state that held during it. */
  const advance = (t: number) => {
    const dt = Math.max(0, t - lastAt)
    if (wasVisible) {
      visibleMs += dt
      if (focused === true) focusedVisibleMs += dt
      if (lastInputAt !== undefined) {
        const idleFrom = Math.max(
          lastAt,
          lastInputAt + ENGAGEMENT_IDLE_AFTER_MS,
        )
        if (t > idleFrom) idleVisibleMs += t - idleFrom
      }
    }
    if (wasHovering) hoverMs += dt
    if (t > lastAt) lastAt = t
  }

  /** Re-derive edge-triggered state after a change. */
  const settle = (t: number) => {
    for (const owner of owners.values())
      if (owner.placement === 'pinned' || owner.visible !== undefined)
        visibilityKnown = true
    const visible = isVisible()
    if (!visible && wasVisible) leftAt = t
    if (visible && visibleAt === undefined) visibleAt = t
    wasVisible = visible
    const hovering = isHovering()
    if (hovering && !wasHovering) {
      hoverCount += 1
      if (firstHoverAt === undefined) firstHoverAt = t
    }
    wasHovering = hovering
  }

  const update = (change: (t: number) => void) => {
    const t = now()
    advance(t)
    change(t)
    settle(t)
  }

  const emit = (record: unknown) => {
    const parsed = parseAdEngagement(record)
    if (!parsed) return
    try {
      options.send(parsed)
    } catch {
      // fire-and-forget: a failed report is a missing row, never a broken ad
    }
  }

  const maybeDone = () => {
    if (doneReported || !flushed || !postClickSettled) return
    doneReported = true
    try {
      options.onDone?.()
    } catch {
      // registry bookkeeping only
    }
  }

  const maybeSendPostClick = () => {
    // Held until a record carrying this impression is out, so the server
    // always sees the row it is asked to merge into first.
    if (!recordOut || !postClick || postClickSent) return
    postClickSent = true
    emit({ v: AD_ENGAGEMENT_VERSION, impUrl, postClick })
  }

  const settlePostClick = (
    value: NonNullable<AdEngagement['postClick']> | undefined,
  ) => {
    if (postClickSettled) return
    postClickSettled = true
    if (postClickTimer !== null) {
      clearTimer(postClickTimer)
      postClickTimer = null
    }
    if (value && Object.keys(value).length > 0) postClick = value
    maybeSendPostClick()
    maybeDone()
  }

  const armPostClick = (t: number) => {
    // Only a terminal known to be focused at the click can lose focus to the
    // browser; anything else leaves `postClick` absent (unknown).
    if (!focusSupported || focused !== true) return
    postClickSettled = false
    postClickClickAt = t
    postClickLeft = false
    postClickTimer = setTimer(() => {
      postClickTimer = null
      settlePostClick({ browserOpened: false })
    }, POST_CLICK_LEAVE_GRACE_MS)
  }

  const postClickFocus = (next: boolean) => {
    if (postClickSettled) return
    const t = now()
    if (!next && !postClickLeft) {
      postClickLeft = true
      if (postClickTimer !== null) clearTimer(postClickTimer)
      postClickTimer = setTimer(
        () => {
          postClickTimer = null
          settlePostClick({ browserOpened: true })
        },
        Math.max(0, POST_CLICK_WATCH_MS - (t - postClickClickAt)),
      )
    } else if (next && postClickLeft) {
      settlePostClick(
        defined({
          browserOpened: true,
          returnMs: clampMs(t - postClickClickAt),
        }),
      )
    }
  }

  const build = (exit: EngagementExit | undefined): AdEngagement =>
    defined({
      v: AD_ENGAGEMENT_VERSION,
      impUrl,
      visibleAtMs:
        visibilityKnown && visibleAt !== undefined
          ? clampMs(visibleAt - mountAt)
          : undefined,
      visibleMs: visibilityKnown ? clampMs(visibleMs) : undefined,
      focusedVisibleMs:
        visibilityKnown && focusSupported
          ? clampMs(focusedVisibleMs)
          : undefined,
      hoverCount: count(hoverCount),
      hoverMs: clampMs(hoverMs),
      firstHoverMs:
        firstHoverAt === undefined
          ? undefined
          : clampMs(firstHoverAt - mountAt),
      sentMessageDuringExposure: sentMessage,
      exit,
      yielded:
        yieldCount > 0
          ? defined({
              reason: yieldReason,
              count: count(yieldCount),
              hiddenMs: heldEver
                ? clampMs(
                    heldHiddenMs +
                      (hiddenSince !== undefined
                        ? Math.max(0, lastAt - hiddenSince)
                        : 0),
                  )
                : undefined,
            })
          : undefined,
      truncated,
      endedBy,
      idleVisibleMs:
        visibilityKnown && lastInputAt !== undefined
          ? clampMs(idleVisibleMs)
          : undefined,
      reentries: visibilityKnown ? count(reentries) : undefined,
      keysDuringExposure:
        visibilityKnown && options.countsKeys
          ? bucketCount(keysVisible)
          : undefined,
      click: currentClick(),
    }) as AdEngagement

  /**
   * The click block to report. A late click on an impression the server
   * already holds a click for keeps that first click's features and adds to
   * its count: the record describes the FIRST click and counts the rest.
   */
  function currentClick(): EngagementClickBlock | undefined {
    if (clickOnly && priorClick) {
      if (clickCount === 0) return priorClick
      return defined({
        ...priorClick,
        // the late click went out the moment it landed
        atClick: true,
        count: count((priorClick.count ?? 1) + clickCount),
      })
    }
    return click ? defined({ ...click, count: count(clickCount) }) : undefined
  }

  /** The record so far, with no `exit`; tracking continues. */
  const sendCheckpoint = () => {
    if (flushed) return
    advance(now())
    emit(build(undefined))
    markRecordOut()
    maybeSendPostClick()
  }

  const exposureCheckpoint = () => {
    if (!checkpoints || flushed || exposureCheckpointed) return
    exposureCheckpointed = true
    sendCheckpoint()
  }

  /** click-only: `{ v, impUrl, click }`, when the count moved since the last. */
  const sendLateClick = () => {
    if (clickCount === 0 || clickCount === emittedClickCount) return
    emittedClickCount = clickCount
    emit({ v: AD_ENGAGEMENT_VERSION, impUrl, click: currentClick() })
    markRecordOut()
    maybeSendPostClick()
  }

  const tracker: EngagementTracker = {
    impUrl,
    get owners() {
      return owners.size
    },
    get flushed() {
      return flushed
    },
    get recordOut() {
      return recordOut
    },
    get done() {
      return flushed && postClickSettled
    },
    get clickBlock() {
      return currentClick()
    },
    attach(owner, placement) {
      if (flushed || owners.has(owner)) return
      update(() => {
        owners.set(owner, { placement, visible: undefined, hovering: false })
      })
    },
    detach(owner) {
      if (flushed || !owners.has(owner)) return
      update(() => {
        owners.delete(owner)
      })
    },
    setVisible(owner, visible) {
      const state = owners.get(owner)
      if (
        flushed ||
        !state ||
        state.placement !== 'measured' ||
        state.visible === visible
      )
        return
      const before = isVisible()
      update((t) => {
        state.visible = visible
        // Only a MEASUREMENT brings a card back: a pinned card remounting
        // inside the release grace is a redraw, not a re-entry.
        if (!before && visible === true && leftAt !== undefined && t > leftAt)
          reentries += 1
      })
      // Scrolled out of the transcript viewport after being seen: the
      // exposure that matters most has happened, and a transcript card may
      // never unmount, so its record goes out now.
      if (before && !isVisible()) exposureCheckpoint()
    },
    hover(owner, hovering) {
      const state = owners.get(owner)
      if (flushed || !state) return
      update(() => {
        if (hovering) pointerMoved = true
        state.hovering = hovering
      })
    },
    click(detail = {}) {
      if (flushed) return
      update((t) => {
        clickCount += 1
        if (click) return
        click = defined({
          msSinceMount: clampMs(t - mountAt),
          msSinceVisible:
            visibilityKnown && visibleAt !== undefined
              ? clampMs(t - visibleAt)
              : undefined,
          msSinceRotation: clampMs(t - rotatedAt),
          pointerMovedOver: pointerMoved,
          windowFocused:
            focusSupported && focused !== null ? focused : undefined,
          region: detail.region,
          modifier: detail.modifier,
          // A click-only tracker and a checkpointing one both send this click
          // the moment it lands (below); the flush's `click` replaces it in
          // the server's shallow merge, so it must say so too.
          atClick: clickOnly || checkpoints ? true : undefined,
        })
        armPostClick(t)
      })
      if (clickOnly) {
        // the first late click goes out now; later ones ride the flush
        if (clickCount === 1) sendLateClick()
      } else if (checkpoints && !clickCheckpointed) {
        clickCheckpointed = true
        sendCheckpoint()
      }
    },
    terminalFocus(next) {
      if (flushed) {
        focusSupported = true
        focused = next
      } else
        update(() => {
          focusSupported = true
          focused = next
        })
      postClickFocus(next)
    },
    messageSent() {
      if (flushed || owners.size === 0) return
      sentMessage = true
    },
    userInput() {
      if (flushed) return
      update((t) => {
        lastInputAt = t
      })
    },
    keystroke() {
      if (flushed || !isVisible()) return
      keysVisible = Math.min(MAX_COUNT, keysVisible + 1)
    },
    truncated(value) {
      if (flushed || truncated !== undefined) return
      truncated = value
    },
    hide(reason, held) {
      if (flushed || clickOnly) return
      update((t) => {
        yieldReason = reason
        yieldCount += 1
        if (held && hiddenSince === undefined) {
          heldEver = true
          hiddenSince = t
        }
      })
    },
    unhide() {
      if (flushed || hiddenSince === undefined) return
      update((t) => {
        heldHiddenMs += Math.max(0, t - (hiddenSince ?? t))
        hiddenSince = undefined
      })
    },
    endedBy(value) {
      if (flushed || clickOnly) return
      endedBy = value
    },
    checkpoint() {
      exposureCheckpoint()
    },
    flush(exit) {
      if (flushed) return
      if (clickOnly) {
        flushed = true
        owners.clear()
        wasVisible = false
        wasHovering = false
        sendLateClick()
        maybeSendPostClick()
        maybeDone()
        return
      }
      advance(now())
      const record = build(exit)
      flushed = true
      owners.clear()
      wasVisible = false
      wasHovering = false
      emit(record)
      markRecordOut()
      maybeSendPostClick()
      maybeDone()
    },
    closePostClick() {
      if (postClickSettled) return
      settlePostClick(postClickLeft ? { browserOpened: true } : undefined)
    },
    snapshot(exit) {
      if (clickOnly)
        return parseAdEngagement({
          v: AD_ENGAGEMENT_VERSION,
          impUrl,
          click: currentClick(),
        })
      const saved = {
        visibleMs,
        focusedVisibleMs,
        hoverMs,
        idleVisibleMs,
        lastAt,
      }
      if (!flushed) advance(now())
      const record = build(exit)
      visibleMs = saved.visibleMs
      focusedVisibleMs = saved.focusedVisibleMs
      hoverMs = saved.hoverMs
      idleVisibleMs = saved.idleVisibleMs
      lastAt = saved.lastAt
      return parseAdEngagement(record)
    },
  }
  return tracker
}

// ------------------------------------------------------------------ registry

export interface EngagementRegistryEnv extends EngagementTimers {
  now: () => number
  send: (record: AdEngagement) => void
  focus: () => FocusState
  releaseGraceMs?: number
  /**
   * Exposure before a live impression sends its checkpoint; defaults to
   * {@link ENGAGEMENT_CHECKPOINT_MS}. `null` turns every checkpoint off
   * (the leave-the-viewport and first-click ones too).
   */
  checkpointAfterMs?: number | null
  /** ms since the user's last input; null/undefined = unknown. */
  idleMs?: () => number | null | undefined
  /** The composer's keystrokes are forwarded through `keystroke()`. */
  countsKeys?: boolean
  /** An impression's main record went out (its flush). */
  onFlushed?: (impUrl: string) => void
  /**
   * A live impression's first record (a checkpoint) went out, so the server
   * has its row while the card is still tracking.
   */
  onRecordOut?: (impUrl: string) => void
}

/**
 * `flushed`: the main record is out. `recorded`: still live, but a checkpoint
 * is out, so the server has the row a merge record needs. `live`: no record
 * yet. `unknown`: never tracked this process.
 */
export type EngagementStatus = 'unknown' | 'live' | 'recorded' | 'flushed'

export interface EngagementHandle {
  readonly impUrl: string
  readonly tracker: EngagementTracker
  /** @internal ownership token */
  readonly token: object
}

export interface EngagementRegistry {
  /**
   * Start (or join, or resume) the record for `impUrl`. A redraw of an
   * impression whose record already went out (a creative rotated back into
   * the slot) is not a new impression: it gets a click-only handle, so a
   * click on it still reaches that impression's record. `null` only when
   * there is no impUrl.
   */
  mount(
    impUrl: string | undefined,
    placement: OwnerPlacement,
  ): EngagementHandle | null
  /** The owning card went away; the flush follows the grace unless it remounts. */
  unmount(handle: EngagementHandle): void
  /** Slots swapped creatives: `from` left on a rotation, `to` arrived. */
  noteSlotSwap(from: readonly string[], to: readonly string[]): void
  /**
   * The slot keeps `impUrl` to show again after another ad leaves the screen:
   * the record stays open (no release grace, no flush) while it is not drawn,
   * and the hidden time is counted. A no-op for an impression not tracked or
   * already flushed.
   */
  hold(impUrl: string, reason: AdYieldReason): void
  /** The held impression may show again; an undrawn one then ends as usual. */
  release(impUrl: string): void
  /** A drawn impression gave way for good (it will unmount now). */
  noteYield(impUrl: string, reason: AdYieldReason): void
  /**
   * A drawn impression came off screen because it was shown too long
   * (`common/src/ads/ad-expiry.ts`): it ends as `rotation` with
   * `endedBy: 'expired'`. Call before the slot swaps it out.
   */
  noteExpired(impUrl: string): void
  terminalFocus(focused: boolean): void
  messageSent(): void
  userInput(): void
  keystroke(): void
  status(impUrl: string): EngagementStatus
  /**
   * The CLI is quitting: flush every live record now, as `exit` for a card
   * still on screen (a card inside its release grace keeps the exit it was
   * given), and settle every post-click watch. Idempotent.
   */
  closeAll(exit?: EngagementExit): void
  /** Test seam. */
  reset(): void
  readonly size: number
}

interface Entry {
  tracker: EngagementTracker
  /** the release grace after the last owner left */
  timer: TimerHandle | null
  /** the exit decided when the last owner left, while the grace runs */
  pendingExit: EngagementExit | null
  /** the bounded-exposure checkpoint */
  checkpointTimer: TimerHandle | null
  /** a click-only redraw of an already-flushed impression */
  late: boolean
  /** kept by its slot to show again: no release grace while undrawn */
  held: boolean
}

export function decideExit(input: {
  rotatedAway: boolean
  msSinceMessageSent: number | undefined
  /** Taken off for being shown too long: a rotation with no successor. */
  expired?: boolean
}): EngagementExit {
  if (input.rotatedAway || input.expired) return 'rotation'
  if (
    input.msSinceMessageSent !== undefined &&
    input.msSinceMessageSent >= 0 &&
    input.msSinceMessageSent <= NEW_MESSAGE_EXIT_WINDOW_MS
  )
    return 'new_message'
  return 'unmount'
}

export function createEngagementRegistry(
  env: EngagementRegistryEnv,
): EngagementRegistry {
  const setTimer = env.setTimer ?? defaultSetTimer
  const clearTimer = env.clearTimer ?? defaultClearTimer
  const grace = env.releaseGraceMs ?? ENGAGEMENT_RELEASE_GRACE_MS
  const checkpointAfterMs =
    env.checkpointAfterMs === null
      ? null
      : (env.checkpointAfterMs ?? ENGAGEMENT_CHECKPOINT_MS)

  const entries = new Map<string, Entry>()
  // Click-only redraws of impressions in `finished`, keyed the same way.
  const lateEntries = new Map<string, Entry>()
  const finished = new Set<string>()
  const rotatedAway = new Set<string>()
  const expired = new Set<string>()
  const swappedInAt = new Map<string, number>()
  // The click block each clicked impression has reported, for late clicks.
  const clicks = new Map<string, EngagementClickBlock>()
  // Flushed click-only trackers displaced by a newer redraw of the same
  // impression while their post-click watch still waits for focus.
  const settling = new Set<EngagementTracker>()
  let lastMessageAt: number | undefined

  const safely = (fn: () => void) => {
    try {
      fn()
    } catch {
      // engagement is additive; it must never break the ad it measures
    }
  }

  const remember = <T>(
    memo: Set<string> | Map<string, T>,
    key: string,
    value?: T,
    max = MAX_SWAP_MEMO,
  ) => {
    // re-inserting moves the key to the newest end
    memo.delete(key)
    if (memo.size >= max) {
      const oldest = memo.keys().next().value
      if (oldest !== undefined) memo.delete(oldest)
    }
    if (memo instanceof Map) memo.set(key, value as T)
    else memo.add(key)
  }

  const clearTimers = (entry: Entry) => {
    if (entry.timer !== null) {
      clearTimer(entry.timer)
      entry.timer = null
    }
    if (entry.checkpointTimer !== null) {
      clearTimer(entry.checkpointTimer)
      entry.checkpointTimer = null
    }
  }

  /** Every tracker that can still use a broadcast. */
  const trackers = (): EngagementTracker[] => [
    ...[...entries.values()].map((entry) => entry.tracker),
    ...[...lateEntries.values()].map((entry) => entry.tracker),
    ...settling,
  ]

  const finish = (impUrl: string, entry: Entry, exit: EngagementExit) => {
    clearTimers(entry)
    entry.pendingExit = null
    const map = entry.late ? lateEntries : entries
    if (!entry.late) {
      finished.add(impUrl)
      rotatedAway.delete(impUrl)
      expired.delete(impUrl)
    }
    safely(() => entry.tracker.flush(exit))
    safely(() => {
      const click = entry.tracker.clickBlock
      if (click) remember(clicks, impUrl, click, MAX_CLICK_MEMO)
    })
    if (!entry.late) safely(() => env.onFlushed?.(impUrl))
    if (entry.tracker.done && map.get(impUrl) === entry) map.delete(impUrl)
  }

  const create = (
    impUrl: string,
    late: boolean,
    map: Map<string, Entry>,
  ): Entry => {
    const rotatedAt = swappedInAt.get(impUrl)
    swappedInAt.delete(impUrl)
    const created: Entry = {
      tracker: undefined as never,
      timer: null,
      pendingExit: null,
      checkpointTimer: null,
      late,
      held: false,
    }
    let idleMsAtMount: number | undefined
    try {
      idleMsAtMount = env.idleMs?.() ?? undefined
    } catch {
      idleMsAtMount = undefined
    }
    created.tracker = createEngagementTracker({
      impUrl,
      now: env.now,
      focus: env.focus(),
      rotatedAt,
      idleMsAtMount,
      countsKeys: env.countsKeys,
      checkpoints: !late && checkpointAfterMs !== null,
      ...(late ? { clickOnly: { priorClick: clicks.get(impUrl) } } : {}),
      setTimer,
      clearTimer,
      send: env.send,
      // the final record reports through `onFlushed` instead
      ...(late
        ? {}
        : {
            onRecordOut: () => {
              if (!created.tracker.flushed)
                safely(() => env.onRecordOut?.(impUrl))
            },
          }),
      onDone: () => {
        settling.delete(created.tracker)
        if (map.get(impUrl) === created && created.timer === null)
          map.delete(impUrl)
      },
    })
    if (!late && checkpointAfterMs !== null)
      created.checkpointTimer = setTimer(() => {
        created.checkpointTimer = null
        // inside the release grace the final record is a second away
        if (created.timer !== null) return
        safely(() => created.tracker.checkpoint())
      }, checkpointAfterMs)
    map.set(impUrl, created)
    return created
  }

  /** The last owner left: decide the exit now, flush after the grace. */
  const startRelease = (impUrl: string, entry: Entry) => {
    const exit = decideExit({
      rotatedAway: rotatedAway.has(impUrl),
      expired: expired.has(impUrl),
      msSinceMessageSent:
        lastMessageAt === undefined ? undefined : env.now() - lastMessageAt,
    })
    entry.pendingExit = exit
    entry.timer = setTimer(() => {
      entry.timer = null
      finish(impUrl, entry, exit)
    }, grace)
  }

  return {
    get size() {
      return entries.size + lateEntries.size
    },
    mount(impUrl, placement) {
      if (!impUrl) return null
      const late = finished.has(impUrl)
      const map = late ? lateEntries : entries
      const token = {}
      let entry = map.get(impUrl)
      if (entry && entry.tracker.flushed) {
        // a click-only redraw whose own record went out, still watching for
        // focus to come back: it keeps hearing focus, and this fresh redraw
        // gets a fresh click-only tracker
        settling.add(entry.tracker)
        entry = undefined
      }
      if (!entry) {
        entry = create(impUrl, late, map)
      } else if (entry.timer !== null) {
        // a remount inside the grace resumes the same record
        clearTimer(entry.timer)
        entry.timer = null
        entry.pendingExit = null
      }
      const tracker = entry.tracker
      safely(() => tracker.attach(token, placement))
      return { impUrl, tracker, token }
    },
    unmount(handle) {
      const entry = [
        entries.get(handle.impUrl),
        lateEntries.get(handle.impUrl),
      ].find((candidate) => candidate?.tracker === handle.tracker)
      if (!entry || entry.tracker.flushed) return
      safely(() => entry.tracker.detach(handle.token))
      if (entry.tracker.owners > 0 || entry.timer !== null || entry.held)
        return
      // The exit is decided NOW, from what is known at the moment the last
      // owner left; the flush waits out the grace for a remount.
      startRelease(handle.impUrl, entry)
    },
    hold(impUrl, reason) {
      const entry = entries.get(impUrl)
      if (!entry || entry.tracker.flushed) return
      // the owner may already have left: its grace is cancelled, not run
      if (entry.timer !== null) {
        clearTimer(entry.timer)
        entry.timer = null
        entry.pendingExit = null
      }
      if (!entry.held) safely(() => entry.tracker.hide(reason, true))
      entry.held = true
    },
    release(impUrl) {
      const entry = entries.get(impUrl)
      if (!entry || !entry.held) return
      entry.held = false
      safely(() => entry.tracker.unhide())
      if (
        !entry.tracker.flushed &&
        entry.tracker.owners === 0 &&
        entry.timer === null
      )
        startRelease(impUrl, entry)
    },
    noteYield(impUrl, reason) {
      const entry = entries.get(impUrl)
      if (!entry || entry.tracker.flushed || entry.held) return
      safely(() => entry.tracker.hide(reason, false))
    },
    noteExpired(impUrl) {
      const entry = entries.get(impUrl)
      if (!entry || entry.tracker.flushed) return
      remember(expired, impUrl)
      safely(() => entry.tracker.endedBy('expired'))
    },
    noteSlotSwap(from, to) {
      const incoming = new Set(to)
      for (const impUrl of from)
        if (impUrl && !incoming.has(impUrl) && !finished.has(impUrl))
          remember(rotatedAway, impUrl)
      const outgoing = new Set(from)
      for (const impUrl of to) {
        if (!impUrl || outgoing.has(impUrl)) continue
        rotatedAway.delete(impUrl)
        // a flushed impression rotating back in is remembered too: a late
        // click on it is timed from this swap
        remember(swappedInAt, impUrl, env.now())
      }
    },
    terminalFocus(focused) {
      for (const tracker of trackers())
        safely(() => tracker.terminalFocus(focused))
    },
    messageSent() {
      lastMessageAt = env.now()
      for (const tracker of trackers()) safely(() => tracker.messageSent())
    },
    userInput() {
      for (const tracker of trackers()) safely(() => tracker.userInput())
    },
    keystroke() {
      for (const tracker of trackers()) safely(() => tracker.keystroke())
    },
    status(impUrl) {
      if (finished.has(impUrl)) return 'flushed'
      const entry = entries.get(impUrl)
      if (!entry) return 'unknown'
      return entry.tracker.recordOut ? 'recorded' : 'live'
    },
    closeAll(exit = 'window_close') {
      for (const map of [entries, lateEntries])
        for (const [impUrl, entry] of [...map]) {
          if (!entry.tracker.flushed)
            finish(
              impUrl,
              entry,
              entry.tracker.owners > 0 ? exit : (entry.pendingExit ?? exit),
            )
          clearTimers(entry)
          safely(() => entry.tracker.closePostClick())
          map.delete(impUrl)
        }
      for (const tracker of [...settling])
        safely(() => tracker.closePostClick())
      settling.clear()

        clearTimers(entry)
      entries.clear()
      lateEntries.clear()
      finished.clear()
      rotatedAway.clear()
      swappedInAt.clear()

/**
 * A transcript card is on screen when any of its rows lies inside the
 * scrollbox viewport. `undefined` when either side cannot be measured.
 */
export function rowsIntersect(
  card: { top: number; height: number } | null,
  viewport: { top: number; bottom: number } | null,
): boolean | undefined {
  if (!card || !viewport) return undefined
  if (
    !Number.isFinite(card.top) ||
    !Number.isFinite(card.height) ||
    card.height <= 0
  )
    return undefined
  return card.top < viewport.bottom && card.top + card.height > viewport.top
}

/**
 * How long a transcript card's rows must stay inside the viewport, with the
 * terminal not known to be unfocused, before its impression is reported.
 */
export const TRANSCRIPT_IMPRESSION_DWELL_MS = 1_000

export interface ViewDwellGate {
  /**
   * Feed one measurement. Returns `true` exactly once: on the first update at
   * which the card has been continuously seen for the dwell.
   *
   * `visible: undefined` (unmeasurable: no viewport registered, or a node
   * that cannot be measured) counts as seen, so a layout the check cannot
   * read still reports what it draws. `focused: undefined` (the terminal has
   * never reported focus) counts as focused. Only a definite `false` on
   * either side breaks the run and restarts the dwell.
   */
  update(now: number, visible: boolean | undefined, focused: boolean | undefined): boolean
  readonly fired: boolean
}

export function createViewDwellGate(
  dwellMs: number = TRANSCRIPT_IMPRESSION_DWELL_MS,
): ViewDwellGate {
  let since: number | undefined
  let fired = false
  return {
    update(now, visible, focused) {
      if (fired) return false
      if (visible === false || focused === false || !Number.isFinite(now)) {
        since = undefined
        return false
      }
      since ??= now
      if (now - since < dwellMs) return false
      fired = true
      return true
    },
    get fired() {
      return fired
    },
  }
}

/**
 * ONE AD ON SCREEN AT A TIME: what the chat's two main ad units hold and
 * show, so every other unit can give way.
 *
 * The two main units are the slot above the input (the dock) and the
 * end-of-answer card in the transcript. The rules:
 *
 * 1. A PAIR FROM TWO SUPPLIERS: the ad network always wins.
 *    - network dock + network card in view: the dock goes blank (the card
 *      outranks it);
 *    - network dock + our card: OUR CARD is not drawn
 *      (`suppressOurCards`), and the card request carries
 *      `networkAdInView` so the server answers with the network or nothing;
 *    - our dock + network card in view: our dock goes blank;
 *    - our dock + our card in view: the dock goes blank.
 *    A blank dock requests nothing, pauses its rotation clock and shows the
 *    ad it held when the card leaves the view.
 * 2. Only the newest answer's card is drawn (`keepNewestResponseAd`).

/**
 * The end-of-answer cards after `messageId` received `ad`: only that answer's
 * card is kept, unless a NEWER answer (later in `answerOrder`) already has
 * one, or the answer is no longer in the transcript (`/new`, a clear), in
 * which case the late card is dropped and nothing drawn changes. Returns the
 * next map and the drawn cards that gave way.
 */
export function keepNewestResponseAd<T>(
  prev: Record<string, T[]>,
  messageId: string,
  ad: T,
  answerOrder: readonly string[],
): { next: Record<string, T[]>; superseded: T[]; dropped: boolean } {
  const incoming = answerOrder.indexOf(messageId)
  if (incoming < 0) return { next: prev, superseded: [], dropped: true }
  const newer = Object.keys(prev).some(
    (id) => id !== messageId && answerOrder.indexOf(id) > incoming,
  )
  if (newer) return { next: prev, superseded: [], dropped: true }
  const superseded: T[] = []
  for (const [id, ads] of Object.entries(prev))
    if (id !== messageId) superseded.push(...ads)
  return {
    next: { [messageId]: [...(prev[messageId] ?? []), ad] },
    superseded,
    dropped: false,
  }
}

/**
 * The end-of-answer cards without the ones that have expired
 * (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`): an expired card is taken off and never drawn
 * again. Returns `prev` itself when nothing expired, so the caller can skip
 * the state write.
 */
export function dropExpiredResponseAds<T extends { impUrl: string }>(
  prev: Record<string, T[]>,
  isExpired: (impUrl: string) => boolean,
): { next: Record<string, T[]>; expired: T[] } {
  const expired: T[] = []
  const next: Record<string, T[]> = {}
  for (const [id, ads] of Object.entries(prev)) {
    const kept = ads.filter((ad) => {
      if (!isExpired(ad.impUrl)) return true
      expired.push(ad)
      return false
    })
    if (kept.length > 0) next[id] = kept
  }
  return expired.length === 0 ? { next: prev, expired } : { next, expired }
}

/**
 * One partner slot's ad, or null.
 *
 * FOUR REFUSALS, all of them the same answer to the caller -- null, draw
 * nothing -- because a partner slot has no fallback. Every other slot in the
 * CLI can fall back to another network, our own book or the house promo; this
 * one is one advertiser's chrome, and the only honest thing to put there when
 * the deal is not serving is nothing at all.
 *
 *  - ADS ARE OFF for this session (`/ads:disable`, a subscription, BYOK). The
 *    caller gates on this too; it is re-checked here because a held answer
 *    must not outlive the setting that allowed it.
 *  - THE POLICY did not name this placement. The deal is not live for this
 *    session and the server would refuse the request anyway, so asking would
 *    spend a round trip to be told so.
 *  - NO `impUrl`. An ad that cannot be acknowledged cannot be billed, and an
 *    unbillable partner impression is one we would be giving away.
 *  - NOT OUR OWN INVENTORY. The client-side echo of the serving fence
 *    (`dropForeignPartnerFills`): only the first-party CPM leg carries this
 *    deal, and a fill from anywhere else in an advertiser's colours is the
 *    one thing this format may never draw.
 */
export async function getPartnerAd(
  placementId: string,
  deps: PartnerAdDeps = productionDeps,
): Promise<AdResponse | null> {
  if (!deps.adsEnabled()) return null
  const authToken = deps.authToken()
  if (!authToken) return null
  syncCacheOwner(authToken)

  const answer = held.get(placementId)
  // A held fill that has been on screen too long is not served again: the
  // slot asks for a new one (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`).
  if (
    answer &&
    deps.now() < answer.expiresAt &&
    !(answer.ad && isAdExpired(answer.ad.impUrl))
  )
    return answer.ad
  const pending = inFlight.get(placementId)
  if (pending) return pending

  const request = (async (): Promise<AdResponse | null> => {
    const announced = await deps.announcedPlacements()
    if (!announced.includes(placementId)) return null
    const data = await deps.fetchAuction(placementId)
    // Advertiser-written text is drawn in the terminal: no escape sequences.
    const ad = data?.ads?.[0] ? sanitizeTerminalStrings(data.ads[0]) : undefined
    if (!ad?.impUrl) return null
    const provider = data?.provider ?? ad.provider
    if (provider !== 'first_party') return null
    return {
      ...ad,
      provider,
      placementId,
      // Receipt stamp for `renderDelayMs` (COD-365): the answer is in hand,
      // the row is not yet on screen.
      receivedAtMs: deps.now(),
    }
  })()
    .catch((err) => {
      logger.debug({ err, placementId }, '[ads] Failed to fetch partner ad')
      return null
    })
    .then((ad) => {
      inFlight.delete(placementId)
      // A no-fill is held for the same window as a fill. Without that, a slot
      // the deal is paced out of would re-auction on every keystroke in the
      // composer -- the busiest slot in the app asking hardest precisely when
      // there is nothing to give it.
      held.set(placementId, { ad, expiresAt: deps.now() + PARTNER_AD_TTL_MS })
      return ad
    })

  inFlight.set(placementId, request)
  return request
}

/**
 * THE COMPOSER INTENT for one draft (the `CLI-Intent` placement): the
 * composer partner ad the server judged the draft to be about, or null. Replaces the keyword triggers the
 * composer row used to read.
 *
 * NOT HELD, unlike {@link getPartnerAd}: each draft is its own question, and
 * the caller paces them (`useComposerIntentAd`, the shared scheduler). The
 * same refusals as a partner slot, plus one: a fill on any slot that is not
 * one of the CLI's composer partner slots is never drawn as this row.
 */
export async function getComposerIntentAd(
  draft: string,
  deps: ComposerIntentDeps = composerIntentDeps,
): Promise<AdResponse | null> {
  if (!deps.adsEnabled()) return null
  const authToken = deps.authToken()
  if (!authToken) return null
  syncCacheOwner(authToken)
  try {
    const announced = await deps.announcedPlacements()
    if (!announced.includes(CLI_COMPOSER_INTENT_PLACEMENT_ID)) return null
    const data = await deps.fetchIntent(draft)
    // Advertiser-written text is drawn in the terminal: no escape sequences.
    const ad = data?.ads?.[0] ? sanitizeTerminalStrings(data.ads[0]) : undefined
    if (!ad?.impUrl) return null
    const provider = data?.provider ?? ad.provider
    if (provider !== 'first_party') return null
    if (!ad.placementId || !CLI_COMPOSER_INTENT_PLACEMENTS.has(ad.placementId))
      return null
    return { ...ad, provider, receivedAtMs: deps.now() }
  } catch (err) {
    logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Failed to fetch composer intent ad')
    return null
  }
}

/**
 * Acknowledge a partner impression, at most once per held answer.
 *
 * Always the first-party transport, because a partner fill is always our own
 * inventory -- {@link getPartnerAd} refuses anything else -- and that is the
 * path with the retry and the render-delay header every other Freebuff ad
 * measures itself with.
 */
export function recordPartnerImpression(ad: AdResponse): void {
  // Called on insertion: the row's expiry clock starts at its first draw.
  noteAdDrawn(ad.impUrl)
  if (!ad.impUrl || ackedImpressions.has(ad.impUrl)) return
  const authToken = getAuthToken()
  if (!authToken) return
  ackedImpressions.add(ad.impUrl)

  const renderDelayMs = renderDelaySinceReceipt(ad)
  const dispatched = dispatchFirstPartyViewAcknowledgement(
    ad.provider,
    {
      token: ad.impUrl,
      url: `${WEBSITE_URL}/api/v1/ads/impression`,
      init: {
        method: 'POST',
        headers: {
          'Content-Type': 'application/json',
          Authorization: `Bearer ${authToken}`,
          'User-Agent': getCliAdRequestUserAgent(),
          ...clientEnvironmentHeaders(),
        },
        body: JSON.stringify({
          impUrl: ad.impUrl,
          mode: useChatStore.getState().agentMode,
          userAgent: getAdUserAgent(),
          os: getAdDeviceInfo().os,
        }),
      },
      surface: 'cli_chat',
      placementId: ad.placementId ?? 'unknown',
      clientFamily: 'cli',
      ...(renderDelayMs !== undefined ? { renderDelayMs } : {}),
    },
    (observation) => {
      const telemetry = createFirstPartyViewAckTelemetry(observation)
      if (telemetry) {
        enqueueClientLog({
          level: 'info',
          event: AnalyticsEvent.ADS_FIRST_PARTY_VIEW_ACK,
          message: 'First-party view acknowledgement',
          data: telemetry,
        })
      }
    },
  )
  if (!dispatched) {
    // Unreachable while `getPartnerAd` refuses a foreign fill, and left as a
    // log rather than a second transport: a partner impression we cannot bill
    // is one to notice, not one to send another way.
    ackedImpressions.delete(ad.impUrl)
    logger.debug(
      { placementId: ad.placementId, provider: ad.provider },
      '[ads] Partner fill was not first-party; impression not acknowledged',
    )
  }
}

/**
 * One display placement's served ad, or null: ads off, signed out, the policy
 * did not announce the id (no request is made), no fill, or a fill that does
 * not parse as a first-party display ad for this id.
 */
export async function getDisplayAd(
  placementId: string,
  deps: DisplayAdDeps = productionDeps,
): Promise<CliServedSponsor | null> {
  if (!isDisplayAdPlacementId(placementId)) return null
  if (!deps.adsEnabled()) return null
  const authToken = deps.authToken()
  if (!authToken) return null
  syncCacheOwner(authToken)

  const answer = held.get(placementId)
  // A held fill that has been on screen too long is not served again: the
  // slot asks for a new one (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`).
  if (
    answer &&
    deps.now() < answer.expiresAt &&
    !(answer.sponsor && isAdExpired(answer.sponsor.served.impUrl))
  )
    return answer.sponsor
  const pending = inFlight.get(placementId)
  if (pending) return pending

  const request = (async (): Promise<CliServedSponsor | null> => {
    const announced = await deps.announcedPlacements()
    if (!announced.includes(placementId)) return null
    const data = await deps.fetchAuction(placementId)
    const raw = data?.ads?.[0]
    if (!raw) return null
    // Advertiser-written text is drawn in the terminal: no escape sequences.
    const ad = parseServedDisplayAd(sanitizeTerminalStrings(raw), placementId, {
      provider: data?.provider,
    })
    if (!ad) return null
    return {
      ...servedDisplayAdToSponsor(ad),
      landingUrl: ad.url,
      receivedAtMs: deps.now(),
    }
  })()
    .catch((err) => {
      logger.debug({ err, placementId }, '[ads] Failed to fetch display ad')
      return null
    })
    .then((sponsor) => {
      inFlight.delete(placementId)
      held.set(placementId, {
        sponsor,
        expiresAt: deps.now() + DISPLAY_AD_TTL_MS,
      })
      return sponsor
    })

  inFlight.set(placementId, request)
  return request
}

import { createComposerIntentScheduler } from '@codebuff/common/ads/composer-intent-scheduler'
import { useEffect, useRef, useState } from 'react'

import { useAdExpired } from './ad-expiry-clock'
import { getComposerIntentAd } from './partner-ads'

import type { AdResponse } from '../hooks/use-gravity-ad'
import type { ComposerIntentScheduler } from '@codebuff/common/ads/composer-intent-scheduler'

/**
 * THE COMPOSER ROW, decided by the server: which composer partner ad, if any,
 * the draft is about (`getComposerIntentAd`). Replaces the keyword triggers
 * the row used to read.
 *
 * Paced by the shared scheduler -- asked only after a 1s idle on a draft of 3+
 * characters, one request in flight, the newest waiting draft sent when it
 * returns. While `enabled` is false (a menu, a form or a review screen owns
 * the input) drafts are not asked about and nothing is drawn, but the
 * scheduler and what it has been told survive, so closing a menu does not
 * re-ask the same draft.
 */
export function useComposerIntentAd(
  draft: string,
  enabled: boolean,
): AdResponse | null {
  const [ad, setAd] = useState<AdResponse | null>(null)
  const scheduler = useRef<ComposerIntentScheduler | null>(null)

  useEffect(() => {
    const next = createComposerIntentScheduler<AdResponse>({
      request: (text) => getComposerIntentAd(text),
      onAnswer: setAd,
    })
    scheduler.current = next
    return () => {
      next.dispose()
      if (scheduler.current === next) scheduler.current = null
    }
  }, [])

  useEffect(() => {
    if (enabled) scheduler.current?.update(draft)
  }, [draft, enabled])

  // Disabled (a menu, a form, or an ad-network ad on screen): a draft waiting
  // for its idle timer is not asked about, a late answer is not shown, and
  // the last row is forgotten, so reopening never redraws a stale row. An
  // answer already asked for stays remembered by the scheduler and is shown
  // again, with no new request, if the same draft is still there.
  useEffect(() => {
    if (enabled) return
    scheduler.current?.update('')
    setAd(null)
  }, [enabled])

  // Shown too long (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`): taken off until the next answer.
  const expired = useAdExpired(ad?.impUrl)
  return enabled && !expired ? ad : null
}

  deps: AdClickDeps = liveAdClickDeps,
): Promise<AdClickOutcome> {
  if (!ad.clickUrl) return 'ignored'
  let opened = false
  if (!deps.isRemote()) {
    try {
      opened = await deps.open(ad.clickUrl)
    } catch {
      opened = false
    }
  }
  if (opened) {
    onOpened()
    return 'opened'
  }
  deps.showLink(ad)
  return 'link_shown'
}

/**
 * Calculate evenly distributed column widths that sum exactly to availableWidth.
 * Distributes remainder pixels across the first N columns so there's no gap.
 */
function columnWidths(count: number, availableWidth: number): number[] {
  const base = Math.floor(availableWidth / count)
  const remainder = availableWidth - base * count
  return Array.from({ length: count }, (_, i) => base + (i < remainder ? 1 : 0))
}

/**
 * A single ad card: full-width above the input ({@link SingleAdBanner}),
 * content-width when interspersed inside an assistant response
 * (BlocksRenderer), and in a row of columns on the landing screen
 * ({@link ChoiceAdBanner}). Manages its own hover state.
 *
 * Impressions are deduped per impUrl in the ads hook. A pinned card (dock,
 * landing screen) reports its impression on mount and on ad rotation. An
 * inline transcript card reports it only once its rows have stayed inside the
 * transcript viewport for the dwell (`useAdEngagement`'s `onSeen`), or on a
 * click, whichever comes first.
 *
 * The `link` variant is the one-line inline link ad (`inlineStyle: 'link'`,
 * placement `Inline-Link-After-Agent-CLI`): the copy underlined, then a dim
 * "Ad". It is measured, seen, yielded to and clicked exactly as the inline
 * card is; only the drawing differs.
 */
export const AdCard: React.FC<{
  ad: AdResponse
  width: number
  variant?: 'card' | 'inline' | 'link'
  onClick?: (ad: AdResponse) => void
  onImpression?: (ad: AdResponse) => void
}> = ({ ad, width, variant = 'card', onClick, onImpression }) => {
  const baseTheme = useTheme()
  const brand = greptileTerminalColors(ad)
  const theme = brand
    ? { ...baseTheme, foreground: brand.ink, muted: brand.ink, primary: brand.ink }
    : baseTheme
  const [isHovered, setIsHovered] = useState(false)

  // Both transcript variants: the card and the link.
  const isInline = variant === 'inline' || variant === 'link'
  useEffect(() => {
    if (isInline) return
    onImpression?.(ad)
  }, [ad, onImpression, isInline])
  // One ad on screen at a time: our own units read whether a card is drawn.
  useEffect(() => {
    if (!isInline) return
    const drawn = { impUrl: ad.impUrl, provider: ad.provider }
    // Its expiry clock starts at the first draw (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`).
    noteAdDrawn(ad.impUrl)
    reportInlineCardDrawn(drawn, true)
    return () => reportInlineCardDrawn(drawn, false)
  }, [ad.impUrl, ad.provider, isInline])

  // Laid out before the early return below, so the engagement hook can judge
  // truncation from exactly what is drawn.
  const inlineLayout =
    variant === 'inline' ? getInlineAdLayout(ad, width) : null
  if (inlineLayout) {
    inlineLayout.title = truncateToWidth(
      inlineLayout.title,
      Math.max(0, width - 8 - AD_REPORT_CONTROL_COLUMNS),
    )
  }
  // The link's text, fitted to the row beside its " Ad" and report control.
  const linkText =
    variant === 'link'
      ? inlineLinkText(
          ad,
          Math.min(
            CLI_INLINE_LINK_MAX_CHARS,
            width - INLINE_LINK_DISCLOSURE.length - AD_REPORT_CONTROL_COLUMNS,
          ),
        )
      : null
  const card = variant === 'card' ? getCardAdLayout(ad, width) : null
  if (card?.headline) {
    card.headline = truncateToWidth(
      card.headline,
      Math.max(0, width - 8 - AD_REPORT_CONTROL_COLUMNS),
    )
  }
  // An inline card scrolls with the transcript and is measured; the dock and
  // the landing screen's columns sit outside any scrollbox.
  const engagement = useAdEngagement(ad.impUrl, {
    placement: isInline ? 'measured' : 'pinned',
    truncated: linkText !== null
      ? layoutTruncated([
          [inlineLinkText(ad, Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER), linkText],
        ])
      : inlineLayout
      ? layoutTruncated([
          [
            (ad.title ?? '').trim() ||
              getAdDisplayLabel({ title: '', url: ad.url ?? '' }).text,
            inlineLayout.title,
          ],
          [ad.adText, inlineLayout.description],
        ])
      : card
        ? layoutTruncated([
            [ad.title, card.headline],
            [ad.adText, card.description],
          ])
        : undefined,
    ...(isInline
      ? {
          onSeen: () => onImpression?.(ad),
          // One ad on screen at a time: the dock gives way while this card
          // is in the transcript viewport.
          onVisibility: (visible: boolean | undefined) =>
            reportInlineCardVisibility(ad, visible),
        }
      : {}),
  })

  const buttonProps = {
    ref: engagement.ref,
    onClick: (event?: unknown) => {
      if (!ad.clickUrl) return
      engagement.onClick(event)
      // A click is proof the card was on screen: an inline card clicked
      // before its dwell elapsed reports its impression now (deduped).
      onImpression?.(ad)
      void openAdClick(ad, () => onClick?.(ad))
    },
    onMouseOver: () => {
      setIsHovered(true)
      engagement.onHover(true)
    },
    onMouseOut: () => {
      setIsHovered(false)
      engagement.onHover(false)
    },
  }

  if (linkText !== null) {
    return (
      <Button
        {...buttonProps}
        style={{
          width,
          height: 1,
          flexDirection: 'row',
          overflow: 'hidden',
        }}
      >
        <text
          style={{
            fg: isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.link,
            flexShrink: 1,
            wrapMode: 'none',
          }}
          attributes={TextAttributes.UNDERLINE}
        >
          {linkText}
        </text>
        <box style={{ flexDirection: 'row', flexShrink: 0 }}>
          <text
            style={{ fg: theme.muted, wrapMode: 'none' }}
            attributes={TextAttributes.DIM}
          >
            {INLINE_LINK_DISCLOSURE}
          </text>
          <AdReportButton ad={{ ...ad, surface: 'cli' }} color={theme.muted} />
        </box>
      </Button>
    )
  }

  if (inlineLayout) {
    const accentColor = isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.muted
    return (
      <Button
        {...buttonProps}
        style={{
          width,
          height: INLINE_AD_CARD_HEIGHT,
          backgroundColor: brand?.background,
          borderStyle: 'single',
          borderColor: accentColor,
          customBorderChars: BORDER_CHARS,
          paddingLeft: 1,
          paddingRight: 1,
          flexDirection: 'column',
          overflow: 'hidden',
        }}
      >
        <box
          style={{
            width: '100%',
            height: 1,
            flexDirection: 'row',
            justifyContent: 'space-between',
            overflow: 'hidden',
          }}
        >
          <text
            style={{
              fg: isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.foreground,
              flexShrink: 1,
              wrapMode: 'none',
            }}
            attributes={TextAttributes.BOLD}
          >
            {inlineLayout.title}
          </text>
          <box style={{ flexDirection: 'row', flexShrink: 0 }}>
            <text style={{ fg: theme.muted, wrapMode: 'none' }}>
              {INLINE_AD_DISCLOSURE}
            </text>
            <AdReportButton ad={{ ...ad, surface: 'cli' }} color={theme.muted} />
          </box>
        </box>
        <box
          style={{
            width: '100%',
            height: 1,
            flexDirection: 'row',
            justifyContent: 'space-between',
            columnGap: INLINE_AD_GAP,
            overflow: 'hidden',
          }}
        >
          <text style={{ fg: theme.muted, flexShrink: 1, wrapMode: 'none' }}>
            {inlineLayout.description}
          </text>
          {inlineLayout.label && (
            <text
              style={{
                fg: accentColor,
                flexShrink: 0,
                wrapMode: 'none',
              }}
              attributes={TextAttributes.UNDERLINE}
            >
              {inlineLayout.label + INLINE_AD_LINK_SUFFIX}
            </text>
          )}
        </box>
      </Button>
    )
  }

  if (!card) return null

  return (
    <Button
      {...buttonProps}
      style={{
        width,
        height: AD_CARD_HEIGHT,
        backgroundColor: brand?.background,
        borderStyle: 'single',
        borderColor: isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.muted,
        customBorderChars: BORDER_CHARS,
        paddingLeft: 1,
        paddingRight: 1,
        flexDirection: 'column',
      }}
    >
      {/* The disclosure rides whichever row is first, so it is never below the
          fold of a card whose body has shrunk to one line. */}
      {card.headline ? (
        <box
          style={{
            flexDirection: 'row',
            justifyContent: 'space-between',
            alignItems: 'flex-start',
            height: 1,
            overflow: 'hidden',
          }}
        >
          <text
            style={{
              fg: isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.foreground,
              flexShrink: 1,
              wrapMode: 'none',
            }}
            attributes={TextAttributes.BOLD}
          >
            {card.headline}
          </text>
          <box style={{ flexDirection: 'row', flexShrink: 0 }}>
            <text style={{ fg: theme.muted }}>{' Ad'}</text>
            <AdReportButton ad={{ ...ad, surface: 'cli' }} color={theme.muted} />
          </box>
        </box>
      ) : null}
      <box
        style={{
          flexDirection: 'row',
          justifyContent: 'space-between',
          alignItems: 'flex-start',
          height: card.descriptionLines,
          overflow: 'hidden',
        }}
      >
        <text style={{ fg: theme.muted, flexShrink: 1 }}>
          {card.description}
        </text>
        {card.headline ? null : (
          <box style={{ flexDirection: 'row', flexShrink: 0 }}>
            <text style={{ fg: theme.muted }}>{' Ad'}</text>
            <AdReportButton ad={{ ...ad, surface: 'cli' }} color={theme.muted} />
          </box>
        )}
      </box>
      <box style={{ flexGrow: 1 }} />
      {/* Bottom: CTA + domain */}
      <box
        style={{
          flexDirection: 'row',
          columnGap: 1,
          alignItems: 'center',
          height: 1,
          overflow: 'hidden',
        }}
      >
        <text
          style={{
            fg: brand?.background ?? INVERTED_CTA_FG,
            bg: isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.muted,
            attributes: TextAttributes.BOLD,
          }}
        >
          {` ${card.ctaText} `}
        </text>
        {card.labelText ? (
          <text
            style={{
              fg: theme.muted,
              wrapMode: 'none',
              attributes:
                card.labelVariant === 'domain'
                  ? TextAttributes.UNDERLINE
                  : TextAttributes.BOLD,
            }}
          >
            {card.labelText}
          </text>
        ) : null}
      </box>
    </Button>
  )
}

/**
 * The V2 resting dock (COD-457): `Sponsored · Brand` on the label row, headline
 * and description on the left, a bordered CTA box on the right.
 *
 * Still exactly {@link AD_CARD_HEIGHT} rows, because the landing screen
 * subtracts that from the model picker's budget. Below
 * `MIN_INLINE_WIDTH_WITH_DESTINATION` this delegates to {@link AdCard}
 * verbatim — narrow terminals keep the layout the house-ad width budget was
 * measured against, and expansion still works there.
 *
 * Nothing here depends on hover. Hover tints the border and inverts the CTA
 * box, exactly as the card always did; expansion is a click or the chord.
 */
export const DockAdCard: React.FC<{
  ad: AdResponse
  width: number
  expanded?: boolean
  chordHint?: string
  onToggle?: () => void
  onClick?: (ad: AdResponse, from: DockClickOrigin) => void
  onImpression?: (ad: AdResponse) => void
}> = ({
  ad,
  width,
  expanded = false,
  chordHint,
  onToggle,
  onClick,
  onImpression,
}) => {
  const baseTheme = useTheme()
  const brand = greptileTerminalColors(ad)
  const theme = brand
    ? { ...baseTheme, foreground: brand.ink, muted: brand.ink, primary: brand.ink }
    : baseTheme
  const [isHovered, setIsHovered] = useState(false)
  const [isCtaHovered, setIsCtaHovered] = useState(false)

  const layout = useMemo(
    () => getDockAdLayout(ad, width, { chordHint, collapsed: !expanded }),
    [ad, width, chordHint, expanded],
  )

  useEffect(() => {
    if (layout.mode !== 'dock') return
    onImpression?.(ad)
  }, [ad, onImpression, layout.mode])

  // The narrow fallback renders `AdCard`, which tracks itself.
  const engagement = useAdEngagement(ad.impUrl, {
    placement: 'pinned',
    enabled: layout.mode === 'dock',
    truncated:
      layout.mode === 'dock'
        ? layoutTruncated([
            [ad.title, layout.headline],
            [ad.adText, layout.description],
            [(ad.cta ?? '').trim() || 'Learn more', layout.ctaText],
          ])
        : undefined,
  })

  // Hover is read from the tint state: the children's over/out bubble to the
  // box and React batches an out-then-over into no change at all.
  const { onHover: reportHover } = engagement
  useEffect(() => {
    reportHover(isHovered)
  }, [isHovered, reportHover])

  if (layout.mode === 'card') {
    // The narrow fallback keeps today's card AND today's click semantics: the
    // whole card opens the landing page, and the dock's toggle is reached from
    // the chord instead. Two different meanings for one click at 40 columns
    // would be worse than no expansion affordance at all.
    return (
      <AdCard
        ad={ad}
        width={width}
        onClick={(clicked) => onClick?.(clicked, 'dock')}
        onImpression={onImpression}
      />
    )
  }

  const accentColor = isHovered ? theme.primary : theme.muted

  return (
    // A plain box, NOT a Button. The CTA used to be a Button nested inside the
    // dock's own Button, and OpenTUI mouse events propagate: one press on the
    // CTA opened the advertiser's URL *and* toggled the panel, recording a
    // false expansion. Rather than rely on stopping propagation through the
    // shared Button, the two clickable regions are siblings and never nest.
    // Hover still tints the whole border, because mouse events bubble UP to
    // this box from either child.
    <box
      onMouseOver={() => setIsHovered(true)}
      onMouseOut={() => setIsHovered(false)}
      style={{
        width,
        height: AD_CARD_HEIGHT,
        backgroundColor: brand?.background,
        borderStyle: 'single',
        borderColor: accentColor,
        customBorderChars: BORDER_CHARS,
        paddingLeft: 1,
        paddingRight: 1,
        flexDirection: 'row',
        overflow: 'hidden',
      }}
    >
      {/* The copy block is the toggle. */}
      <Button
        onClick={() => onToggle?.()}
        style={{
          width: layout.copyWidth,
          flexDirection: 'column',
          overflow: 'hidden',
        }}
      >
        <box
          style={{
            height: 1,
            flexDirection: 'row',
            justifyContent: 'space-between',
            overflow: 'hidden',
          }}
        >
          <box style={{ flexDirection: 'row', flexShrink: 1 }}>
            <text style={{ fg: theme.primary, wrapMode: 'none' }}>
              {layout.sponsored}
            </text>
            <text style={{ fg: theme.muted, wrapMode: 'none' }}>
              {DOCK_LABEL_SEPARATOR}
            </text>
            <text
              style={{
                fg: theme.foreground,
                flexShrink: 1,
                wrapMode: 'none',
                attributes:
                  layout.brandVariant === 'domain'
                    ? TextAttributes.UNDERLINE
                    : undefined,
              }}
            >
              {layout.brand}
            </text>
          </box>
          {layout.chordHint ? (
            <text style={{ fg: theme.muted, flexShrink: 0, wrapMode: 'none' }}>
              {layout.chordHint}
            </text>
          ) : null}
          <AdReportButton ad={{ ...ad, surface: 'cli' }} color={theme.muted} />
        </box>
        {layout.headline ? (
          <text
            style={{ fg: theme.foreground, height: 1, wrapMode: 'none' }}
            attributes={TextAttributes.BOLD}
          >
            {layout.headline}
          </text>
        ) : null}
        <text
          style={{ fg: theme.muted, height: layout.descriptionLines }}
        >
          {layout.description}
        </text>
      </Button>
      <box style={{ flexGrow: 1 }} />
      {/* A SIBLING of the toggle, never a child: the CTA opens the landing
          page and must not also expand the panel. */}
      <Button
        onClick={(event?: unknown) => {
          if (!ad.clickUrl) return
          engagement.onClick(event, 'cta')
          void openAdClick(ad, () => onClick?.(ad, 'dock'))
        }}
        onMouseOver={() => setIsCtaHovered(true)}
        onMouseOut={() => setIsCtaHovered(false)}
        style={{
          width: layout.ctaBoxWidth,
          height: 3,
          borderStyle: 'single',
          borderColor: theme.primary,
          customBorderChars: BORDER_CHARS,
          paddingLeft: 1,
          paddingRight: 1,
          flexShrink: 0,
        }}
      >
        <text
          style={{
            fg: isCtaHovered ? brand?.background ?? INVERTED_CTA_FG : theme.primary,
            bg: isCtaHovered ? theme.primary : undefined,
            wrapMode: 'none',
          }}
          attributes={TextAttributes.BOLD}
        >
          {layout.ctaText + INLINE_AD_LINK_SUFFIX}
        </text>
      </Button>
    </box>
  )
}

/**

              wrapMode: 'none',
            }}
            attributes={TextAttributes.BOLD}
          >
            {panel.ctaText + INLINE_AD_LINK_SUFFIX}
          </text>

        </box>
      </box>
    </box>
  )

/** The panel's own close control. Exported so the tmux capture can assert it. */
export const DOCK_CLOSE_LABEL = '[ Close ]'

/**
 * Rows the composer and its surrounding chrome are never allowed to lose.
 *
 * The dock itself is {@link AD_CARD_HEIGHT}; the rest is the bordered input
 * box plus the hint row beneath it. Deliberately generous: overestimating
 * costs a bullet row, and underestimating covers the input box, which is the
 * one outcome the spec forbids outright.
 */
export const DOCK_COMPOSER_RESERVED_ROWS = AD_CARD_HEIGHT + 6

/**
 * Rows the dock keeps, blank, while it gives way to an end-of-answer card:
 * the collapsed dock's own height at every width and arm (the detail panel
 * collapses when the dock goes blank), so nothing above or below it moves
 * and a resize cannot leave it stale.
 */
export const DOCK_BLANK_ROWS = AD_CARD_HEIGHT

/** The dock's rows while it gives way: blank, its collapsed height. */
export const DockBlankRows: React.FC = () => (
  <box style={{ height: DOCK_BLANK_ROWS, flexShrink: 0 }} />
)

/**
 * How many rows the panel may occupy above the dock at this terminal height.
 *
 * Pure and tested, for the same reason the degradation ladder is: "never
 * covers the composer at 24 rows" cannot be seen in a snapshot of a tall
 * terminal.
 */
export function dockPanelRowBudget(terminalHeight: number): number {
  return Math.max(0, Math.floor(terminalHeight) - DOCK_COMPOSER_RESERVED_ROWS)
}

/**
 * The rotating ad pinned above the chat input box. Rerenders (and fires a new
 * impression) each time the hook rotates `ads[0]`.
 *
 * `arm` is the sticky CLI dock arm (COD-457). `control` renders the card
 * exactly as it did before this issue — same component, same props, same five
 * rows — so the control arm is byte-identical to today by construction rather
 * than by a test that could drift.
 */

/**
 * Resolve the ad policy once per process, for whoever asks first.
 *
 * The dock hook used to be the only caller and could simply fetch in an
 * effect. The partner rows need the same body (they cannot request a slot the
 * policy has not named) and mount on a keystroke, so the fetch is shared
 * here rather than raced: two fetches would be two assignments of a sticky
 * arm, which is the one thing a sticky assignment must not do.
 */
export function resolveAdPolicy(): Promise<CliDockArm> {
  if (policyRequest) return policyRequest
  const request = fetchDockArm().then((arm) => {
    // Recorded before any render so the very next ad request reports the arm
    // this session will actually draw.
    sessionDockArm = arm
    return arm
  })
  // A policy resolved with no credentials is not this session's policy: it is
  // the answer for a logged-out process, which asks for nothing and is told
  // nothing. Memoizing it would pin the session to it through the login that
  // usually follows.
  if (getAuthToken()) policyRequest = request
  return request
}

  sessionPartnerPlacementIds = null

/** A placement-id list off the policy body: non-empty strings, else `[]`. */

  return Array.isArray(value)
    ? value.filter(
        (id): id is string => typeof id === 'string' && id.length > 0,

/**
 * The policy fetch is best-effort by design: a CLI that cannot reach the
 * policy route must still show ads, so every failure — network, non-200,
 * unparseable body, an arm string we do not recognise — lands on control. An
 * experiment that fails open into its own treatment arm is not an experiment.
 *
 * The partner and display-ad lists fail the same way, to nothing at all: an
 * older server, an unreachable one and a deal that is not live must all leave
 * this client drawing no partner row or served display ad rather than
 * requesting a slot it was never offered.
 */
export async function fetchDockArm(
  fetchImpl: typeof fetch = fetch,
  websiteUrl: string = WEBSITE_URL,
): Promise<CliDockArm> {
  const authToken = getAuthToken()
  if (!authToken) return 'control'
  try {
    const response = await fetchImpl(`${websiteUrl}/api/v1/ads/policy`, {
      method: 'GET',
      headers: { Authorization: `Bearer ${authToken}` },
    })
    if (!response.ok) {
      setAdExpiryPolicy(null)
      return 'control'
    }
    const data = (await response.json()) as {
      dockArm?: unknown
      partnerPlacementIds?: unknown
      displayPlacementIds?: unknown
      inlineAdStyle?: unknown
    }
    // Anything but `link` (an older server, a missing field) is the card.
    sessionInlineAdStyle = data?.inlineAdStyle === 'link' ? 'link' : 'card'
    sessionPartnerPlacementIds = policyPlacementIds(data?.partnerPlacementIds)
    sessionDisplayPlacementIds = policyPlacementIds(data?.displayPlacementIds)
    // How long one ad may stay drawn (`adMaxShownMs`, `adAwayExpiryMs`);
    // an older server sends neither and the defaults apply.
    setAdExpiryPolicy(data)
    return data?.dockArm === 'expandable' ? 'expandable' : 'control'
  } catch (err) {
    logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Failed to fetch dock policy')
    sessionPartnerPlacementIds = []
    sessionDisplayPlacementIds = []
    sessionInlineAdStyle = 'card'
    setAdExpiryPolicy(null)
    return 'control'
  }
}

export function useDockPanel(options: {
  ad: AdResponse | null | undefined
  enabled?: boolean
  /**
   * Whether a panel would actually FIT on this terminal right now, from
   * `getDockPanelLayout(...).fits`. False refuses the expansion outright
   * rather than parking the hook in an open state that renders nothing: on an
   * 18-21 row terminal the budget leaves fewer rows than the smallest panel
   * needs, and accepting the toggle there made the dock look broken — the
   * chord hint vanished and Escape was needed to clear an invisible state.
   */
  canExpand?: boolean
  fetchArm?: () => Promise<CliDockArm>
  now?: () => number
  track?: DockTracker
}): DockPanelState {
  const enabled = options.enabled ?? true
  const now = options.now ?? Date.now
  const trackRef = useRef<DockTracker>(options.track ?? defaultDockTracker)
  trackRef.current = options.track ?? defaultDockTracker
  const track: DockTracker = useCallback(
    (event, properties) => trackRef.current(event, properties),
    [],
  )
  const [arm, setArm] = useState<CliDockArm>('control')
  const [expanded, setExpanded] = useState(false)

  const openedAtRef = useRef(0)
  /**
   * The ad the panel was opened ON, snapshotted beside `openedAtRef`.
   *
   * `adRef` has already advanced to the INCOMING ad by the time the rotation
   * effect collapses, so reporting from it attributed the outgoing ad's dwell
   * to the new ad's `imp_url` — and to `undefined` when the ad simply went
   * away. Collapse telemetry reads this instead.
   */
  const openedAdRef = useRef<AdResponse | null>(null)
  const expansionsFiredRef = useRef(new Set<string>())
  const adRef = useRef<AdResponse | null | undefined>(options.ad)
  adRef.current = options.ad
  const canExpandRef = useRef(options.canExpand ?? true)
  canExpandRef.current = options.canExpand ?? true

  // Once per CLI session. A second fetch could hand the same user a different
  // arm mid-session, which is the one thing a sticky assignment must not do.
  const fetchArmRef = useRef(options.fetchArm)
  fetchArmRef.current = options.fetchArm
  useEffect(() => {
    if (!enabled) return
    let cancelled = false
    void (async () => {
      const resolved = await (fetchArmRef.current?.() ?? resolveAdPolicy())
      if (cancelled) return
      // Recorded before the render so the very next ad request reports the
      // arm this session will actually draw. `resolveAdPolicy` already did
      // this for itself; an INJECTED arm has nobody else to do it.
      sessionDockArm = resolved
      setArm(resolved)
    })()
    return () => {
      cancelled = true
    }
  }, [enabled])

  const collapse = useCallback(
    (method: DockCollapseMethod) => {
      setExpanded((wasOpen) => {
        if (!wasOpen) return false
        const ad = openedAdRef.current
        track(AnalyticsEvent.ADS_DOCK_COLLAPSED, {
          imp_url: ad?.impUrl,
          provider: ad?.provider,
          placement_id: ad?.placementId,
          method,
          dwell_ms: dockDwellMs(openedAtRef.current, now()),
        })
        return false
      })
    },
    [now, track],
  )

  const toggle = useCallback(
    (method: DockExpandMethod) => {
      setExpanded((wasOpen) => {
        if (wasOpen) {
          const opened = openedAdRef.current
          track(AnalyticsEvent.ADS_DOCK_COLLAPSED, {
            imp_url: opened?.impUrl,
            provider: opened?.provider,
            placement_id: opened?.placementId,
            // The chord toggling shut is its own collapse method; a click on
            // the dock body while open is the "outside the panel" case.
            method: method === 'key' ? 'key' : 'outside',
            dwell_ms: dockDwellMs(openedAtRef.current, now()),
          })
          return false
        }
        const ad = adRef.current
        if (!ad) return false
        // Refuse rather than open something that cannot be drawn.
        if (!canExpandRef.current) return false
        openedAtRef.current = now()
        openedAdRef.current = ad
        // Deduped per impUrl per app session: opening the same ad ten times is
        // one expansion, and an expansion is never an impression.
        if (claimDockExpansion(expansionsFiredRef.current, ad.impUrl)) {
          track(AnalyticsEvent.ADS_DOCK_EXPANDED, {
            imp_url: ad.impUrl,
            provider: ad.provider,
            placement_id: ad.placementId,
            method,
          })
        }
        return true
      })
    },
    [now, track],
  )

  // Collapse on rotation and on the ad going away. The user opened THIS ad's
  // details, not the next one's. Keyed on `impUrl`, which is what the ads hook
  // rotates; the panel opening never makes the hook rotate sooner.
  const lastImpUrlRef = useRef<string | undefined>(options.ad?.impUrl)
  useEffect(() => {
    const impUrl = options.ad?.impUrl
    if (impUrl === lastImpUrlRef.current) return
    const hadAd = lastImpUrlRef.current !== undefined
    lastImpUrlRef.current = impUrl
    if (!hadAd) return
    collapse(impUrl === undefined ? 'gone' : 'rotate')
  }, [options.ad?.impUrl, collapse])

  const clickContext = useCallback(
    (from: DockClickOrigin): DockClickContext => {
      const dwellMs =
        from === 'panel' ? dockDwellMs(openedAtRef.current, now()) : 0
      return {
        from,
        dwellMs,
        accidental: from === 'panel' && dwellMs < DOCK_ACCIDENTAL_CLICK_MS,
      }
    },
    [now],
  )

  const expandable = enabled && arm === 'expandable'
  return {
    arm,
    expanded: expandable && expanded,
    expandable,
    toggle,
    collapse,
    clickContext,
  }
}

const AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS = 60 * 1000 // 60 seconds per ad
/**
 * Timer jitter a rotation tick tolerates: a tick is due once the last rotation
 * is at least the interval minus this old. Every rotation re-phases the timer,
 * so ticks normally land a full interval after the last rotation.
 */
export const AD_ROTATION_TICK_SLACK_MS = 1_000
const MAX_ADS_AFTER_ACTIVITY = 3 // Show up to 3 ads after last activity, then pause fetching new ads
const ACTIVITY_THRESHOLD_MS = 30_000 // 30 seconds idle threshold for fetching new ads
const MAX_AD_CACHE_SIZE = 50 // Maximum number of ads to keep in cache

/**
 * Whether the terminal's focus lets an ad be fetched or counted. Only a
 * reported `false` blocks: `undefined` means the terminal never reported
 * focus (no DEC 1004 support), which must read as focused, or every such
 * terminal would draw ads it never counts.
 */
export function adFocusAllows(focused: boolean | undefined): boolean {
  return focused !== false
}

/**
 * What one rotation tick of the slot above the input does.
 * - `skip`: nothing (ads off, the slot held by a proposal, or the terminal
 *   unfocused: no fetch, no swap, so no new impression either).
 * - `fetch`: auction a new ad (the activity rule: fewer than
 *   {@link MAX_ADS_AFTER_ACTIVITY} shown since the user last typed, and they
 *   typed within {@link ACTIVITY_THRESHOLD_MS}).
 * - `cache`: no auction; re-show a cached ad if there is one.
 * - `expire`: the drawn ad has been shown too long (`ad-expiry-clock.ts`):
 *   clear the slot. Decided before every reason to skip, so an unfocused
 *   terminal (whose ticks all skip) still loses its ad.
 *
 * After an expiry the slot stays empty until the person is active again
 * (`expiredIdle`): no cached re-show, and the next active tick fetches.
 */
export function rotationTickPlan(params: {
  adsEnabled: boolean
  slotPaused: boolean
  focused: boolean | undefined
  adsShownSinceActivity: number
  userActive: boolean
  /** `Date.now()` of the slot's last rotation; null or absent before any. */
  lastRotationAt?: number | null
  now?: number
  /** The drawn ad has expired under the shared rule. */
  currentExpired?: boolean
  /** The slot was cleared by an expiry and the person has not been active since. */
  expiredIdle?: boolean
}): 'skip' | 'fetch' | 'cache' | 'expire' {
  if (!params.adsEnabled) return 'skip'
  if (params.currentExpired) return 'expire'
  if (params.slotPaused) return 'skip'
  if (!adFocusAllows(params.focused)) return 'skip'
  // A rotation on focus return (or a first fetch run late) may have happened
  // since the last tick: never rotate twice within one interval.
  if (
    params.lastRotationAt != null &&
    params.now !== undefined &&
    params.now - params.lastRotationAt <
      AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS - AD_ROTATION_TICK_SLACK_MS
  )
    return 'skip'
  if (params.adsShownSinceActivity < MAX_ADS_AFTER_ACTIVITY && params.userActive)
    return 'fetch'
  return params.expiredIdle ? 'skip' : 'cache'
}

/**
 * On focus returning: rotate now only when a full interval has passed since
 * the slot last rotated (ticks while unfocused were skipped, not queued).
 */
export function rotationDueOnFocus(params: {
  lastRotationAt: number | null
  now: number
  intervalMs?: number
}): boolean {
  if (params.lastRotationAt === null) return true
  return (
    params.now - params.lastRotationAt >=
    (params.intervalMs ?? AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS)
  )
}

/**
 * Whether an answer's end-of-answer card may be auctioned now: the answer has
 * completed (the renderer only asks then), the terminal is not unfocused, and
 * the answer's end, where the card will draw, is in the transcript viewport.
 * `endVisible: undefined` (unmeasurable) does not hold the request back.
 */
export function inlineRequestDisposition(params: {
  count: number
  focused: boolean | undefined
  endVisible?: boolean
  /**
   * A sponsored offer holds the dock. It is never hidden, so the card waits
   * (retried until the offer has gone, while the answer is still newest).
   */
  offerInDock?: boolean
}): 'request' | 'defer' | 'none' {
  if (params.count <= 0) return 'none'
  if (params.offerInDock) return 'defer'
  if (!adFocusAllows(params.focused)) return 'defer'
  return params.endVisible === false ? 'defer' : 'request'
}

/** Whether the rows `measure` reports lie in the transcript viewport. */
export function answerEndVisible(
  measure: MeasureRows | undefined,
): boolean | undefined {
  if (!measure) return undefined
  try {
    return rowsIntersect(measure(), getAdTranscriptViewport())
  } catch {
    return undefined
  }
}

/**
 * The rotation clock after the slot gave way for `yieldedAt..now`: the
 * hidden time does not count, so the held ad gets the rest of its interval.
 * `firstTickInMs` is when the next rotation is due; null `lastRotationAt`
 * (nothing shown yet) starts a fresh interval.
 */
export function resumeRotationAfterYield(params: {
  lastRotationAt: number | null
  yieldedAt: number
  now: number
  intervalMs?: number
}): { lastRotationAt: number | null; firstTickInMs: number } {
  const interval = params.intervalMs ?? AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS
  if (params.lastRotationAt === null)
    return { lastRotationAt: null, firstTickInMs: interval }
  const hiddenMs = Math.max(0, params.now - params.yieldedAt)
  const shownMs = Math.max(0, params.yieldedAt - params.lastRotationAt)
  return {
    lastRotationAt: params.lastRotationAt + hiddenMs,
    firstTickInMs: Math.max(0, interval - shownMs),
  }
}

/** The dock giving way, while it does: since when, and what it holds. */
export type SlotYieldHold = { since: number; impUrls: string[] }

/**
 * A rotation's answer that landed while the slot had given way (a fetch
 * already in flight when the card came into view): kept, not drawn, and
 * shown when the slot comes back, with no second request. `undefined` is
 * "nothing held"; `null` is a held "clear the slot" (a withheld answer).
 */
export type HeldRotation = AdResponse[] | null | undefined

/**
 * What one rotation answer does to the slot: `show` it now, or keep it
 * `held` because the slot has given way since the request left.
 */
export function deliverRotation(
  ctrl: { heldRotation: HeldRotation },
  show: AdResponse[] | null | undefined,
  yielded: boolean,
): 'show' | 'held' | 'none' {
  if (show === undefined) return 'none'
  if (!yielded) return 'show'
  ctrl.heldRotation = show
  return 'held'
}

/**
 * The slot giving way (`yielded` true) or coming back (false). Returns the
 * next hold. Giving way holds the drawn ads' engagement records open.
 * Coming back, in order: a rotation answer held meanwhile is shown (a full
 * interval from now); else a first fetch skipped meanwhile runs; else the
 * clock resumes with the rest of the held ad's interval. The held records
 * are released after the swap, so an ad replaced meanwhile ends as a
 * rotation.
 */
export function applySlotYield(
  hold: SlotYieldHold | null,
  params: {
    yielded: boolean
    now: number
    /** impUrls the slot draws now. */
    drawnImpUrls: readonly string[]
    /** The slot is started and not hidden. */
    slotActive: boolean
    focused: boolean | undefined
    ctrl: {
      lastRotationAt: number | null
      initialFetchPending: boolean
      heldRotation: HeldRotation
    }
    hold: (impUrl: string) => void
    release: (impUrl: string) => void
    show: (ads: AdResponse[] | null) => void
    runInitialFetch: () => void
    restartRotationTimer: (firstDelayMs?: number) => void
    intervalMs?: number
    /**
     * The held ad expired while hidden (it kept ageing): drop it instead of
     * showing it again.
     */
    heldExpired?: boolean
    expire?: () => void
  },
): SlotYieldHold | null {
  if (params.yielded) {
    if (hold) return hold
    const impUrls = [...params.drawnImpUrls]
    for (const impUrl of impUrls) params.hold(impUrl)
    return { since: params.now, impUrls }
  }
  if (!hold) return null
  const ctrl = params.ctrl
  const held = ctrl.heldRotation
  ctrl.heldRotation = undefined
  if (params.slotActive && held !== undefined) {
    params.show(held)
    ctrl.lastRotationAt = params.now
    params.restartRotationTimer()
  } else if (
    params.slotActive &&
    ctrl.initialFetchPending &&
    adFocusAllows(params.focused)
  ) {
    params.runInitialFetch()
    params.restartRotationTimer()
  } else if (params.slotActive && params.heldExpired && params.expire) {
    params.expire()
    params.restartRotationTimer()
  } else if (params.slotActive) {
    const resumed = resumeRotationAfterYield({
      lastRotationAt: ctrl.lastRotationAt,
      yieldedAt: hold.since,
      now: params.now,
      intervalMs: params.intervalMs,
    })
    ctrl.lastRotationAt = resumed.lastRotationAt
    params.restartRotationTimer(resumed.firstTickInMs)
  }
  for (const impUrl of hold.impUrls) params.release(impUrl)
  return null
}

/**
 * The impUrls on screen for the focus-return path: the dock's ads unless the
 * slot has given way, and every drawn end-of-answer card.
 */
export function drawnAdImpUrls(params: {
  dockAds: readonly AdResponse[] | null
  dockYielded: boolean
  responseAds: Record<string, readonly AdResponse[]>
}): Set<string> {
  const drawn = new Set<string>()
  if (!params.dockYielded)
    for (const ad of params.dockAds ?? []) drawn.add(ad.impUrl)
  for (const list of Object.values(params.responseAds))
    for (const ad of list) drawn.add(ad.impUrl)
  return drawn
}

/** The controller fields the focus-return path reads and writes. */
export type FocusReturnState = {
  lastRotationAt: number | null
  initialFetchPending: boolean
  deferredImpressions: Map<string, AdResponse>
}

/**
 * Focus returning: send impressions held while unfocused for ads still drawn
 * (ads no longer drawn are dropped), run a skipped first fetch or an overdue
 * rotation (re-phasing the rotation timer, so the next regular tick is a full
 * interval later), and retry a card request held while unfocused.
 */
export function handleAdFocusReturn(
  state: FocusReturnState,
  deps: {
    now: number
    /** impUrls of every ad currently drawn (dock and transcript cards). */
    drawnImpUrls: ReadonlySet<string>
    /** The slot above the input is running (started and not hidden). */
    slotActive: boolean
    recordImpression: (ad: AdResponse) => void
    runInitialFetch: () => void
    rotate: () => void
    restartRotationTimer: () => void
    retryDeferredInline: () => void
    intervalMs?: number
  },
): 'initial_fetch' | 'rotate' | 'none' {
  const deferred = [...state.deferredImpressions.values()]
  state.deferredImpressions.clear()
  for (const ad of deferred) {
    if (deps.drawnImpUrls.has(ad.impUrl)) deps.recordImpression(ad)
  }

  let rotated: 'initial_fetch' | 'rotate' | 'none' = 'none'
  if (deps.slotActive) {
    if (state.initialFetchPending) {
      deps.runInitialFetch()
      rotated = 'initial_fetch'
    } else if (
      rotationDueOnFocus({
        lastRotationAt: state.lastRotationAt,
        now: deps.now,
        intervalMs: deps.intervalMs,
      })
    ) {
      deps.rotate()
      rotated = 'rotate'
    }
    if (rotated !== 'none') deps.restartRotationTimer()
  }

  deps.retryDeferredInline()
  return rotated
}

// Ad response type (normalized shape across providers; credits added after impression)
export type AdResponse = {
  adText: string
  title: string
  cta: string
  url: string
  favicon: string
  clickUrl: string
  impUrl: string
  placementId?: string
  provider?: AdProvider
  credits?: number // Set after impression is recorded (in cents)
  /**
   * `Date.now()` when the auction RESPONSE was received (COD-365). The origin
   * of `renderDelayMs` on the impression ack: receipt to card mount, so our
   * own server latency (already on `ads.fetch_completed.duration_ms`) stays
   * out of a client metric. Absent on ads that predate the stamp; the ack
   * then simply omits the delay and the server stores unknown.
   */
  receivedAtMs?: number
  /**
   * Optional expanded creative for the dock's detail panel (COD-457). Only
   * first-party creatives carry these; a Gravity or house ad arrives
   * without them and the panel falls back to `adText`, no bullets, no diagram.
   */
  expandedBody?: string
  bullets?: string[]
  diagram?: string
  /** Explicit server-owned partner designation. */
  partnerBrand?: PartnerBrand
  /**
   * Reviewed fill and ink, `#rrggbb`, for custom partner rows. Ordinary ads
   * use them only with Greptile's explicit partner designation.
   */
  brandColor?: string
  brandInk?: string
  /**
   * Set only on an end-of-answer ad auctioned for the one-line link
   * (`Inline-Link-After-Agent-CLI`); absent means the card. Client-side only.
   */
  inlineStyle?: 'link'
}

/**
 * The one "no inline ads" value. It MUST keep its identity across renders:
 * Chat's layout effect lists `responseAds` as a dependency and writes it into
 * the message-block store, so a fresh `{}` per render (every render whenever
 * ads are hidden) re-ran that effect after every commit, scheduling another
 * store write and a synchronous re-render of every message block. During fast
 * streaming that is the chain of commits-with-pending-updates that React counts
 * toward "Maximum update depth exceeded" (#185).
 */
export const NO_RESPONSE_ADS: Record<string, AdResponse[]> = Object.freeze(
  {},
) as Record<string, AdResponse[]>

export function visibleResponseAds(
  visible: boolean,
  responseAds: Record<string, AdResponse[]>,
): Record<string, AdResponse[]> {
  return visible ? responseAds : NO_RESPONSE_ADS
}

/**
 * Whether the hook neither fetches nor acknowledges. A hidden hook makes no
 * auction, so nothing is served that the screen cannot draw.
 *
 * That matters beyond cost: the ad-signal detector reads `viewed / served`
 * per account (docs/freebuff-ad-signal-detection.md), so an ad fetched for a
 * slot that is not on screen counts against the user. Freebuff ignores the
 * compact-height rule because its chat slot always draws; a caller whose
 * slot can be hidden passes `enabled: false` for exactly as long as it is.
 */
export function shouldHideGravityAds(params: {
  enabled: boolean
  terminalHeight: number
  isFreeMode: boolean
}): boolean {
  const isVeryCompactHeight = params.terminalHeight <= 17
  return !params.enabled || (isVeryCompactHeight && !params.isFreeMode)
}

/**
 * Milliseconds from auction-response receipt to now, or undefined when the ad
 * carries no receipt time. Never negative: a clock that moved backwards is a
 * zero, not a rejection, mirroring the server's clamp.
 */
export function renderDelaySinceReceipt(
  ad: Pick<AdResponse, 'receivedAtMs'>,
  now: number = Date.now(),
): number | undefined {
  if (typeof ad.receivedAtMs !== 'number' || !Number.isFinite(ad.receivedAtMs))
    return undefined
  return Math.max(0, Math.round(now - ad.receivedAtMs))
}

type ChoiceCache = Pick<GravityController, 'choiceCache' | 'choiceCacheIndex'>

// Pure helper: add an ad set to the cache
function addToChoiceCache(ctrl: ChoiceCache, ads: AdResponse[]): void {
  // Deduplicate by checking if any set has the same first impUrl
  const key = ads[0]?.impUrl
  if (key && ctrl.choiceCache.some((set) => set[0]?.impUrl === key)) return
  if (ctrl.choiceCache.length >= MAX_AD_CACHE_SIZE) ctrl.choiceCache.shift()
  ctrl.choiceCache.push(ads)
}

// Pure helper: get the next cached ad set, passing over expired ones (an
// expired impression is never drawn again).
function nextFromChoiceCache(
  ctrl: ChoiceCache,
  isExpired: (impUrl: string) => boolean = () => false,
): AdResponse[] | null {
  for (let tries = 0; tries < ctrl.choiceCache.length; tries++) {
    const set =
      ctrl.choiceCache[ctrl.choiceCacheIndex % ctrl.choiceCache.length]!
    ctrl.choiceCacheIndex =
      (ctrl.choiceCacheIndex + 1) % ctrl.choiceCache.length
    if (!set.some((ad) => isExpired(ad.impUrl))) return set
  }
  return null
}

/**
 * COD-906: the server CHOSE no ad for this request (the new-user ad ramp,
 * docs/experiments.md) -- not a no-fill. Only `{ ads: [], withheld: true }`
 * counts; any other body is read as before.
 */
export function isWithheldAdResponse(data: unknown): boolean {
  if (typeof data !== 'object' || data === null) return false
  const body = data as { ads?: unknown; withheld?: unknown }
  return (
    body.withheld === true && Array.isArray(body.ads) && body.ads.length === 0
  )
}

/** A fetch: served ads, a withheld request, or nothing usable. */
export type AdFetchResult =
  | { ads: AdResponse[] }
  | { withheld: true }
  | null

/**
 * What one rotation tick does with its fetch. Served ads are cached and
 * shown; a withheld request clears the slot (COD-906: re-showing a cached
 * ad would undo the server's decision); a failed or empty fetch falls back
 * to the cache as before, and leaves the slot alone when the cache is empty.
 * `undefined` means "leave the slot as it is".
 */
export function rotationStep(
  ctrl: ChoiceCache,
  result: AdFetchResult,
  isExpired?: (impUrl: string) => boolean,
): { show: AdResponse[] | null | undefined; countsAsShown: boolean } {
  if (result && 'withheld' in result) return { show: null, countsAsShown: false }
  if (result) {
    addToChoiceCache(ctrl, result.ads)
    return { show: result.ads, countsAsShown: true }
  }
  const cachedSet = nextFromChoiceCache(ctrl, isExpired)
  return cachedSet
    ? { show: cachedSet, countsAsShown: true }
    : { show: undefined, countsAsShown: false }
}

/**
 * A streamed LLM answer (possibly still in flight). Other top-level
 * 'ai'-variant messages (bash echoes, system notices, mode dividers) are
 * excluded via the `ai-` id prefix.
 */
export function isAnswerMessage(m: ChatMessage): boolean {
  return (
    !m.parentId && m.variant === 'ai' && m.id.startsWith(AI_MESSAGE_ID_PREFIX)
  )
}

export function isInlineAdEligibleAnswer(m: ChatMessage): boolean {
  return isAnswerMessage(m) && m.metadata?.allowInlineAds === true
}

export function claimAdImpression(
  impressionsFired: Set<string>,
  impUrl: string,
): boolean {
  if (impressionsFired.has(impUrl)) return false
  impressionsFired.add(impUrl)
  return true
}

/**
 * Narrow testable boundary: only our own inventory uses the resilient view
 * acknowledgement transport. Third-party providers retain their legacy pixel
 * acknowledgement path below.
 */
export function dispatchFirstPartyViewAcknowledgement(
  provider: AdProvider | undefined,
  request: Omit<FirstPartyViewAckRequest, 'onAttempt'>,
  onAttempt: NonNullable<FirstPartyViewAckRequest['onAttempt']>,
  acknowledge: typeof acknowledgeFirstPartyView = acknowledgeFirstPartyView,

  if (provider !== 'first_party') return false

  return true
}

/**
 * Send a click report once this ad's view ack has settled, or after
 * `FIRST_PARTY_VIEW_BEFORE_CLICK_WAIT_MS`. Only first-party views are tracked
 * in flight; any other ad, or one whose view already landed, sends at once.
 */
export function sendClickAfterView<T>(
  impUrl: string,
  send: () => Promise<T>,
  viewSettled: (token: string) => Promise<void> = firstPartyViewAckSettled,
): Promise<T> {
  return viewSettled(impUrl).then(send)
}

/**
 * Report a click on any CLI ad.
 *
 * Module-level rather than a closure inside the hook because the PARTNER rows
 * are not the hook's ads and still settle through the same endpoint: one
 * click path means an advertiser's click is counted the same way wherever it
 * was drawn. Never awaited by a caller — a click that waited on our own
 * telemetry before opening the link would be slower than the ad is worth.
 *
 * The report itself waits, bounded, behind this ad's view ack
 * ({@link sendClickAfterView}): callers fire the view first, open the link,
 * and the server still sees the view before the click (ad click IVT).
 */
export function recordAdClick(
  ad: Pick<AdResponse, 'impUrl'> & { url?: string },
  options?: { surface?: AdSurface; dock?: DockClickContext },
): void {
  const authToken = getAuthToken()
  if (!authToken) {
    logger.warn('[ads] No auth token, skipping ad click recording')
    return
  }

  // COD-694: watch for the user's next prompt, the CLI's "came back" signal.
  watchAdClickReturn(ad.impUrl)
  noteAdClicked()
  // COD-757: watch for the agent installing the clicked vendor's package.
  armAdAdoptionWatch(ad.impUrl, ad.url)

  // One id per logical click (COD-365); a repeat POST of the same ad is a
  // new gesture and a new id, and the server answers `alreadyRecorded`.
  const clientEventId = crypto.randomUUID()
  const dock = options?.dock
  const ack = sendClickAfterView(ad.impUrl, () =>
    timedApiCall(() =>
      fetch(`${WEBSITE_URL}/api/v1/ads/click`, {
        method: 'POST',
        headers: {
          'Content-Type': 'application/json',
          Authorization: `Bearer ${authToken}`,
          'User-Agent': getCliAdRequestUserAgent(),
          ...clientEnvironmentHeaders(),
          [FREEBUFF_EVENT_ID_HEADER]: clientEventId,
        },
        body: JSON.stringify({
          impUrl: ad.impUrl,
          clientEventId,
          ...(options?.surface ? { surface: options.surface } : {}),
          // The dock's own fields ride the ACK (COD-457), so the canonical
          // server-side `ads.clicked` carries them and one click stays one
          // event. Emitting a second client-side click here double-counted.
          ...(dock
            ? {
                dockFrom: dock.from,
                dockDwellMs: dock.dwellMs,
                dockAccidentalClick: dock.accidental,
              }
            : {}),
        }),
      }),
    ),
  )
    .then((res) => {
      if (!res.ok) {
        logger.debug({ status: res.status }, '[ads] Failed to record ad click')
      }
    })
    .catch((err) => {
      logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Failed to record ad click')
    })
  // The return report for this click waits for it to be recorded (COD-694).
  trackAdClickAck(ad.impUrl, ack)
}

function trackInlineAdEvent(
  event: AnalyticsEvent,
  properties: Record<string, unknown>,
): void {
  try {
    trackEvent(event, properties)
  } catch (error) {
    // Telemetry must never interfere with fetching or rendering an ad.
    logger.debug({ error, event }, '[ads] Failed to track inline ad event')
  }
}

type GravityAdOptionsBase = {
  enabled?: boolean
  /** Skip the "wait for first user message" gate. Used by the freebuff
   *  landing screen, which has no conversation but still needs ads. */
  forceStart?: boolean
  /** Ad network to request first. The server owns fallback ordering. */
  provider?: AdProvider
  /** Product surface requesting the ad. The server maps this to placements. */
  surface?: AdSurface
  /** Explicit provider placement id for the rotating `ads[0]` slot. */
  slotPlacementId?: string
  /**
   * Stop rotating `ads[0]` while something else holds its slot (the sponsor
   * dock). An ad fetched then would be served and never rendered; ads are
   * fetched only when they can be rendered. Inline ads are unaffected.
   */
  slotPaused?: boolean
  /**
   * The slot gives way to an end-of-answer card in the transcript viewport
   * (one ad on screen at a time): no ad drawn, no fetch, no impression, and
   * the rotation clock stops. When it ends the slot shows the ad it held,
   * with the rest of that ad's interval.
   */
  slotYielded?: boolean
  placementIds?: string[]
}

type GravityAdOptions = GravityAdOptionsBase &

    | {

        inlinePlacementId: string

        inlineLinkPlacementId?: string

/**
 * Fetches the rotating ad slot and, with `inline`, one card per answer, placed
 * after the answer's last content and auctioned once the answer has completed.
 *
 * Nothing is fetched and no impression is sent while the terminal reports
 * itself unfocused (`adFocusAllows`); a terminal that never reports focus
 * counts as focused.
 */
export const useGravityAd = (options?: GravityAdOptions): GravityAdState => {
  const enabled = options?.enabled ?? true
  const forceStart = options?.forceStart ?? false
  const provider: AdProvider = options?.provider ?? 'gravity'
  const surface = options?.surface
  const inline = options?.inline ?? false
  const inlinePlacementId = options?.inlinePlacementId
  const inlineLinkPlacementId = options?.inlineLinkPlacementId
  const slotPlacementId = options?.slotPlacementId
  // Read by the tick through a ref, so pausing needs no timer restart.
  const slotPausedRef = useRef(false)
  slotPausedRef.current = options?.slotPaused ?? false
  const slotYielded = options?.slotYielded ?? false
  const slotYieldedRef = useRef(false)
  slotYieldedRef.current = slotYielded
  const placementIds = options?.placementIds
  const [ads, setAdsState] = useState<AdResponse[] | null>(null)
  // The rotating slot's creatives as last set, so a swap can be told to the
  // engagement registry (COD-757) before React unmounts the old cards: their
  // records then end as `rotation`, not `unmount`.
  const adsRef = useRef<AdResponse[] | null>(null)
  const setAds = (
    next:
      | AdResponse[]
      | null
      | ((cur: AdResponse[] | null) => AdResponse[] | null),
  ): void => {
    const resolved = typeof next === 'function' ? next(adsRef.current) : next
    try {
      adEngagementRegistry().noteSlotSwap(
        (adsRef.current ?? []).map((ad) => ad.impUrl),
        (resolved ?? []).map((ad) => ad.impUrl),
      )
    } catch {
      // engagement must never break rotation
    }
    adsRef.current = resolved
    // The dock draws what it is set to (a yielded slot holds instead).
    for (const ad of resolved ?? []) noteAdDrawn(ad.impUrl)
    setAdsState(resolved)
  }
  const [responseAds, setResponseAds] = useState<Record<string, AdResponse[]>>(
    {},
  )
  const [isLoading, setIsLoading] = useState(false)

  const { terminalHeight } = useTerminalLayout()

  // The live facts the ad client context reads (COD-757).
  const renderer = useRenderer()
  useEffect(() => {
    if (!renderer) return
    return registerAdRenderer(() => ({
      useMouse: renderer.useMouse,
      width: renderer.width,
      height: renderer.height,
    }))
  }, [renderer])
  useEffect(
    () =>
      useChatStore.subscribe((state, prev) =>
        noteAdTurnState(state.isChainInProgress, prev.isChainInProgress),
      ),
    [],
  )
  const shouldHideAds = shouldHideGravityAds({
    enabled,
    terminalHeight,
    isFreeMode: IS_FREEBUFF,
  })

  // Use Zustand selector instead of manual subscription - only rerenders when value changes
  const hasUserMessagedStore = useChatStore((s) =>
    s.messages.some((m) => m.variant === 'user'),
  )
  // forceStart lets callers (e.g. the landing screen) opt out of the
  // "wait for the first user message" gate.
  const shouldStart = forceStart || hasUserMessagedStore
  const shouldStartRef = useRef(shouldStart)
  shouldStartRef.current = shouldStart

  // Single consolidated controller ref
  const ctrlRef = useRef<GravityController>({
    choiceCache: [],
    choiceCacheIndex: 0,
    impressionsFired: new Set(),
    adsShownSinceActivity: 0,
    tickInFlight: false,
    inlineQueue: createLazyResponseAdQueue<AdResponse>(),
    eligibleSlotCounts: new Map(),
    lastRotationAt: null,
    initialFetchPending: false,
    deferredImpressions: new Map(),
    deferredInlineRequests: new Map(),
    deferredInlinePoll: null,
    heldRotation: undefined,
    expiredIdle: false,
  })
  const responseAdsRef = useRef<Record<string, AdResponse[]>>({})
  responseAdsRef.current = responseAds
  // The cards actually drawable (our own left out beside a network dock).
  const drawableResponseAdsRef = useRef<Record<string, AdResponse[]>>({})

  // Ref for the tick function (avoids useCallback dependency issues)
  const tickRef = useRef<() => void>(() => {})
  const initialFetchRef = useRef<() => Promise<void>>(async () => {})
  const requestResponseAdsRef = useRef<
    (messageId: string, count: number, measureEnd?: MeasureRows) => void
  >(() => {})
  const restartRotationTimerRef = useRef<(firstDelayMs?: number) => void>(
    () => {},
  )

  // Ref to track whether ads should be hidden for use in async code
  const shouldHideAdsRef = useRef(shouldHideAds)
  shouldHideAdsRef.current = shouldHideAds

  // Fire impression and update credits (called when showing an ad)
  const recordImpressionOnce = (
    ad: AdResponse,
    options?: { force?: boolean },
  ): void => {
    // Don't record impressions when ads should be hidden
    if (shouldHideAdsRef.current) return

    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    const { impUrl } = ad
    if (ctrl.impressionsFired.has(impUrl)) return
    if (
      impressionDisposition({
        focused: getAdTerminalFocus(),
        force: options?.force,
      }) === 'defer'
    ) {
      ctrl.deferredImpressions.set(impUrl, ad)
      return
    }
    ctrl.deferredImpressions.delete(impUrl)
    if (!claimAdImpression(ctrl.impressionsFired, impUrl)) return

    const recordLocalImpression = async (): Promise<void> => {
      const authToken = getAuthToken()
      if (!authToken) {
        logger.warn('[ads] No auth token, skipping local impression recording')
        return
      }

      // Include mode in request - Freebuff should not grant credits (no balance concept).
      const agentMode = useChatStore.getState().agentMode
      // Measured HERE, at the moment the card is shown, against the receipt
      // stamp `fetchAd` put on the ad (COD-365). The first-party transport
      // sends it as a header; the third-party body carries it too.
      const renderDelayMs = renderDelaySinceReceipt(ad)

      const dispatchedFirstPartyAck = dispatchFirstPartyViewAcknowledgement(
        ad.provider,
        {
          token: impUrl,
          url: `${WEBSITE_URL}/api/v1/ads/impression`,
          init: {
            method: 'POST',
            headers: {
              'Content-Type': 'application/json',
              Authorization: `Bearer ${authToken}`,
              'User-Agent': getCliAdRequestUserAgent(),
              ...clientEnvironmentHeaders(),
            },
            body: JSON.stringify({
              impUrl,
              mode: agentMode,
              userAgent: getAdUserAgent(),
              os: getAdDeviceInfo().os,
            }),
          },
          surface: surface ?? 'cli_chat',
          placementId: ad.placementId ?? slotPlacementId ?? 'unknown',
          clientFamily: 'cli',
          ...(renderDelayMs !== undefined ? { renderDelayMs } : {}),
        },
        (observation) => {
          const telemetry = createFirstPartyViewAckTelemetry(observation)
          if (telemetry) {
            enqueueClientLog({
              level: 'info',
              event: AnalyticsEvent.ADS_FIRST_PARTY_VIEW_ACK,
              message: 'First-party view acknowledgement',
              data: telemetry,
            })
          }
        },
      )
      if (dispatchedFirstPartyAck) {
        return
      }

      // One id per logical event (COD-365). This path has no retry, so one
      // mint per call is one per event; the header is what the server reads.
      const clientEventId = crypto.randomUUID()
      const res = await timedApiCall(() =>
        fetch(`${WEBSITE_URL}/api/v1/ads/impression`, {
          method: 'POST',
          headers: {
            'Content-Type': 'application/json',
            Authorization: `Bearer ${authToken}`,
            'User-Agent': getCliAdRequestUserAgent(),
            ...clientEnvironmentHeaders(),
            [FREEBUFF_EVENT_ID_HEADER]: clientEventId,
          },
          body: JSON.stringify({
            impUrl,
            mode: agentMode,
            // The same shared ad user agent (getAdUserAgent) and OS this ad was
            // auctioned with, so the impression the server records for us
            // matches the auction request.
            userAgent: getAdUserAgent(),
            os: getAdDeviceInfo().os,
            clientEventId,
            ...(renderDelayMs !== undefined ? { renderDelayMs } : {}),
          }),
        }),
      )

      if (!res.ok) {
        logger.debug(
          { status: res.status },
          '[ads] Failed to record local ad impression',
        )
        return
      }

      const data = await res.json()
      if (data.creditsGranted > 0) {
        logger.info(
          { creditsGranted: data.creditsGranted },
          '[ads] Ad impression credits granted',
        )
        // Also update credits in visible ads
        setAds((cur) => {
          if (!cur) return cur
          return cur.map((a) =>
            a.impUrl === impUrl ? { ...a, credits: data.creditsGranted } : a,
          )
        })
      }
    }

    recordLocalImpression().catch((err) => {
      logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Failed to record ad impression')
    })
  }

  // The focus listener is subscribed once; it calls the latest closure.
  const recordImpressionRef = useRef(recordImpressionOnce)
  recordImpressionRef.current = recordImpressionOnce

  const recordClick = (ad: AdResponse, dock?: DockClickContext): void => {
    // A click is proof the ad was on screen. Its view normally went out when
    // it was seen; this sends it now if it never did (deduped otherwise), so
    // the click report below has a view to wait behind.
    recordImpressionOnce(ad, { force: true })
    recordAdClick(ad, {
      ...(surface ? { surface } : {}),
      ...(dock ? { dock } : {}),
    })
  }

  type FetchAdResult = AdFetchResult

  // Fetch an ad via web API
  const fetchAd = async (params?: {
    placementId?: string
    placementIds?: string[]
    /** The other slot shows an ad-network ad: network fills only. */
    networkAdInView?: boolean
  }): Promise<FetchAdResult> => {
    // Don't fetch ads when they should be hidden
    if (shouldHideAdsRef.current) return null
    if (!getAdsEnabled()) return null

    const request = await buildAdAuctionRequest({
      provider,
      ...(surface ? { surface } : {}),
      ...(params?.placementId ? { placementId: params.placementId } : {}),
      ...(params?.placementIds?.length
        ? { placementIds: params.placementIds }
        : {}),
      allowSponsoredRoute: true,
      ...(params?.networkAdInView ? { networkAdInView: true } : {}),
    })
    if (!request) return null

    try {
      const response = await timedAdFetch(() =>
        fetch(request.url, request.init),
      )

      if (!response.ok) {
        let responseBody: unknown
        try {
          const contentType = response.headers.get('content-type') ?? ''
          responseBody = contentType.includes('application/json')
            ? await response.json()
            : await response.text()
        } catch {
          responseBody = 'Unable to parse error response'
        }
        logger.warn(
          { provider, status: response.status, response: responseBody },
          '[ads] Web API returned error',
        )
        return null
      }

      const data = await response.json()

      if (isWithheldAdResponse(data)) return { withheld: true }

      if (
        Array.isArray(data.ads) &&
        data.ads.length > 0 &&
        dropOurFillBesideNetwork(
          { provider: data.provider ?? provider },
          params?.networkAdInView === true,
        )
      ) {
        // Asked for the network only (the other slot shows a network ad)
        // and answered with ours by a server that ignores the hint: never
        // drawn, never counted, not this slot's answer.
        logger.debug(
          { placementId: params?.placementId },
          '[ads] Dropped our own fill beside an ad-network ad',
        )
        return null
      }

      if (Array.isArray(data.ads) && data.ads.length > 0) {
        // Receipt stamp for `renderDelayMs` (COD-365): the response is in
        // hand, the card is not yet on screen.
        const receivedAtMs = Date.now()
        noteAdsServed(data.ads.length, receivedAtMs)
        return {
          // Every string an ad network or advertiser wrote is drawn in the
          // terminal: strip escape sequences and controls here, once, so an
          // OSC 52 / OSC 8 / CSI in a title or body never reaches the screen.
          ads: sanitizeTerminalStrings(data.ads as AdResponse[]).map((ad) => ({
            ...ad,
            provider: data.provider ?? provider,
            receivedAtMs,
          })),
        }
      }
    } catch (err) {
      // `url` names which of the two ad routes threw. Only Bun's own network
      // errors carry the request as `path`, so without it a parse failure or
      // any other throw is unattributable to a host -- and the two routes do
      // not fail alike (2026-10-14: every connection failure in six hours was
      // against freebuff.com's `/api/ads`, none against `/api/v1/ads`).
      logger.error(
        { err, provider, url: request.url },
        '[ads] Failed to fetch ad',
      )
    }

    return null
  }

  // The slot's first fetch. Skipped while the terminal is unfocused; the
  // focus listener runs it when focus returns.
  initialFetchRef.current = async () => {
    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    try {
      if (slotPausedRef.current) return
      if (slotYieldedRef.current || !adFocusAllows(getAdTerminalFocus())) {
        ctrl.initialFetchPending = true
        return
      }
      ctrl.initialFetchPending = false
      ctrl.lastRotationAt = Date.now()
      const networkAdInView = dockRequestNeedsNetworkHint()
      const result = await fetchAd({
        placementId: slotPlacementId,
        placementIds,
        ...(networkAdInView ? { networkAdInView } : {}),
      })
      if (result && 'ads' in result) {
        addToChoiceCache(ctrl, result.ads)
        // The slot may have given way while the request was out: keep the
        // answer for when it comes back rather than set an ad nobody sees.
        if (deliverRotation(ctrl, result.ads, slotYieldedRef.current) === 'show')
          setAds(result.ads)
        ctrl.adsShownSinceActivity = 1
      }
    } finally {
      setIsLoading(false)
    }
  }

  // The dock's drawn ad has been shown too long. A yielded slot draws
  // nothing; its held ad is checked when the slot comes back.
  const slotAdExpired = (): boolean =>
    !slotYieldedRef.current &&
    (adsRef.current ?? []).some((ad) => isAdExpired(ad.impUrl))

  // Clear the slot for an expiry: the records end as `rotation` with
  // `endedBy: 'expired'`, and nothing is re-shown from the cache until the
  // person is active again.
  const expireSlot = (): void => {
    for (const ad of adsRef.current ?? []) noteAdExpiredEngagement(ad.impUrl)
    ctrlRef.current.expiredIdle = true
    setAds(null)
  }

  // Every AD_EXPIRY_CHECK_MS: the dock and the end-of-answer cards.
  const checkExpiry = (): void => {
    if (slotAdExpired()) expireSlot()
    const { next, expired } = dropExpiredResponseAds(
      responseAdsRef.current,
      (impUrl) => isAdExpired(impUrl),
    )
    if (expired.length === 0) return
    for (const ad of expired) {
      ctrlRef.current.deferredImpressions.delete(ad.impUrl)
      noteAdExpiredEngagement(ad.impUrl)
    }
    responseAdsRef.current = next
    setResponseAds(next)
  }
  const checkExpiryRef = useRef(checkExpiry)
  checkExpiryRef.current = checkExpiry

  // Update tick function (uses ref to avoid useCallback dependency issues)
  tickRef.current = () => {
    void (async () => {
      const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
      if (ctrl.tickInFlight) return
      ctrl.tickInFlight = true

      try {
        const now = Date.now()
        const plan = rotationTickPlan({
          adsEnabled: getAdsEnabled(),
          slotPaused: slotPausedRef.current || slotYieldedRef.current,
          focused: getAdTerminalFocus(),
          adsShownSinceActivity: ctrl.adsShownSinceActivity,
          userActive: isUserActive(ACTIVITY_THRESHOLD_MS),
          lastRotationAt: ctrl.lastRotationAt,
          now,
          currentExpired: slotAdExpired(),
          expiredIdle: ctrl.expiredIdle,
        })
        if (plan === 'expire') {
          expireSlot()
          return
        }
        if (plan === 'skip') return
        ctrl.lastRotationAt = now

        const networkAdInView = dockRequestNeedsNetworkHint()
        const result =
          plan === 'fetch'
            ? await fetchAd({
                placementId: slotPlacementId,
                placementIds,
                ...(networkAdInView ? { networkAdInView } : {}),
              })
            : null

        const step = rotationStep(ctrl, result, (impUrl) =>
          isAdExpired(impUrl),
        )
        if (step.countsAsShown) ctrl.adsShownSinceActivity += 1
        // Given way since the request left: held, shown when the slot is back.
        if (deliverRotation(ctrl, step.show, slotYieldedRef.current) === 'show')
          setAds(step.show ?? null)
      } finally {
        ctrl.tickInFlight = false
      }
    })()
  }

  // Retry a held end-of-answer card request: only the latest answer's, and
  // only while it is still the latest (a new prompt drops it, see below).
  // requestResponseAds re-checks focus and visibility and holds it again if
  // either still blocks.
  const retryDeferredInline = (): void => {
    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    if (ctrl.deferredInlineRequests.size === 0) return
    const pending = new Map(ctrl.deferredInlineRequests)
    ctrl.deferredInlineRequests.clear()
    const answers = useChatStore.getState().messages.filter(isAnswerMessage)
    const latest = answers[answers.length - 1]?.id
    if (latest && pending.has(latest)) {
      requestResponseAdsRef.current(
        latest,
        RESPONSE_AD_END_SLOT_COUNT,
        pending.get(latest),
      )
    }
  }
  const retryDeferredInlineRef = useRef(retryDeferredInline)
  retryDeferredInlineRef.current = retryDeferredInline

  // While a card request is held, re-check it on a poll, so scrolling the
  // answer's end into view releases it.
  const stopDeferredInlinePoll = (): void => {
    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    if (ctrl.deferredInlinePoll) clearInterval(ctrl.deferredInlinePoll)
    ctrl.deferredInlinePoll = null
  }
  const ensureDeferredInlinePoll = (): void => {
    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    if (ctrl.deferredInlinePoll) return
    ctrl.deferredInlinePoll = setInterval(() => {
      try {
        retryDeferredInlineRef.current()
      } catch (err) {
        logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Deferred card retry failed')
      }
      if (ctrlRef.current.deferredInlineRequests.size === 0) {
        stopDeferredInlinePoll()
      }
    }, TRANSCRIPT_VISIBILITY_POLL_MS)
    ;(ctrl.deferredInlinePoll as { unref?: () => void }).unref?.()
  }

  // A new prompt drops a held card request: the user has moved on.
  useEffect(() => {
    const stop = subscribeAdUserSend(() => {
      ctrlRef.current.deferredInlineRequests.clear()
      stopDeferredInlinePoll()
    })
    return () => {
      stop()
      stopDeferredInlinePoll()
    }
  }, [])

  // Focus returning (a focus-in report, or a key or submit after a focus-out report).
  useEffect(() => {
    ensureAdTerminalFocusWatch()
    return subscribeAdTerminalFocus((focused) => {
      if (!focused) return
      const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
      try {
        handleAdFocusReturn(ctrl, {
          now: Date.now(),
          drawnImpUrls: drawnAdImpUrls({
            dockAds: adsRef.current,
            dockYielded: slotYieldedRef.current,
            responseAds: drawableResponseAdsRef.current,
          }),
          slotActive:
            shouldStartRef.current &&
            !shouldHideAdsRef.current &&
            !slotYieldedRef.current,
          recordImpression: (ad) => recordImpressionRef.current(ad),
          runInitialFetch: () => void initialFetchRef.current(),
          rotate: () => tickRef.current(),
          restartRotationTimer: () => restartRotationTimerRef.current(),
          retryDeferredInline: () => retryDeferredInlineRef.current(),
        })
      } catch (err) {
        logger.debug({ err }, '[ads] Focus-return handling failed')
      }
    })
  }, [])

  // Reset ads shown counter on user activity
  useEffect(() => {
    if (!getAdsEnabled()) return
    return subscribeToActivity(() => {
      ctrlRef.current.adsShownSinceActivity = 0
      ctrlRef.current.expiredIdle = false
    })
  }, [])

  // The slot giving way to an end-of-answer card and coming back
  // (`applySlotYield`). The held ad's engagement record stays open (not
  // flushed as an unmount) and counts its hidden time.
  const yieldRef = useRef<SlotYieldHold | null>(null)
  useEffect(() => {
    const registry = (): ReturnType<typeof adEngagementRegistry> | null => {
      try {
        return adEngagementRegistry()
      } catch {
        return null
      }
    }
    yieldRef.current = applySlotYield(yieldRef.current, {
      yielded: slotYielded,
      now: Date.now(),
      drawnImpUrls: (adsRef.current ?? []).map((ad) => ad.impUrl),
      slotActive: shouldStartRef.current && !shouldHideAdsRef.current,
      focused: getAdTerminalFocus(),
      ctrl: ctrlRef.current,
      hold: (impUrl) => registry()?.hold(impUrl, 'inline_card_in_view'),
      release: (impUrl) => registry()?.release(impUrl),
      show: (next) => setAds(next),
      runInitialFetch: () => void initialFetchRef.current(),
      restartRotationTimer: (firstDelayMs) =>
        restartRotationTimerRef.current(firstDelayMs),
      heldExpired:
        !slotYielded &&
        (adsRef.current ?? []).some((ad) => isAdExpired(ad.impUrl)),
      expire: () => expireSlot(),
    })
  }, [slotYielded])

  // Start rotation when user sends first message (or immediately if forced).
  useEffect(() => {
    if (!shouldStart || !getAdsEnabled() || shouldHideAds) return

    setIsLoading(true)

    // Fetch first ad immediately, unless the dock already holds the slot.
    void initialFetchRef.current()

    // Start interval for rotation (consistent 60s intervals). A rotation
    // run on focus return restarts it, so the next tick is a full interval
    // after that rotation.
    let id = setInterval(() => tickRef.current(), AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS)
    let first: ReturnType<typeof setTimeout> | null = null
    // With `firstDelayMs`, the next tick comes after that delay (the rest of
    // an interval interrupted by the slot giving way), then every interval.
    restartRotationTimerRef.current = (firstDelayMs?: number) => {
      clearInterval(id)
      if (first) clearTimeout(first)
      first = null
      if (firstDelayMs === undefined) {
        id = setInterval(() => tickRef.current(), AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS)
        return
      }
      first = setTimeout(() => {
        first = null
        tickRef.current()
        id = setInterval(() => tickRef.current(), AD_ROTATION_INTERVAL_MS)
      }, firstDelayMs)
    }

    // Expiry is checked more often than the slot rotates, and also covers
    // the end-of-answer cards.
    const expiryCheck = setInterval(
      () => checkExpiryRef.current(),
      AD_EXPIRY_CHECK_MS,
    )

    return () => {
      clearInterval(id)
      clearInterval(expiryCheck)
      if (first) clearTimeout(first)
      restartRotationTimerRef.current = () => {}
    }
  }, [shouldStart, shouldHideAds, provider, surface, placementIds?.join(',')])

  // Called by BlocksRenderer once an answer has completed, for its single
  // end-of-answer card. One auction per answer at most: the queue counts the
  // attempt, so a no-fill is not retried and a re-render asks for nothing new.
  // Held (and retried) while the terminal is unfocused or the answer's end is
  // off screen, so a card is not fetched where it will not be seen.
  const requestResponseAds = (
    messageId: string,
    rawCount: number,
    measureEnd?: MeasureRows,
  ): void => {
    const count = Math.min(rawCount, RESPONSE_AD_END_SLOT_COUNT)
    if (
      !inline ||
      !inlinePlacementId ||
      count <= 0 ||
      shouldHideAdsRef.current ||
      !getAdsEnabled()
    ) {
      return
    }

    const messages = useChatStore.getState().messages
    const answer = messages.find((m) => m.id === messageId)
    if (!answer || !isInlineAdEligibleAnswer(answer)) {
      return
    }

    const ctrl = ctrlRef.current
    if (
      inlineRequestDisposition({
        count,
        focused: getAdTerminalFocus(),
        endVisible: answerEndVisible(measureEnd),
        offerInDock: slotPausedRef.current,
      }) === 'defer'
    ) {
      ctrl.deferredInlineRequests.set(messageId, measureEnd)
      ensureDeferredInlinePoll()
      return
    }
    ctrl.deferredInlineRequests.delete(messageId)
    // Card or link, decided once per auction. With the CLI gate off this is
    // always the card placement, so the request is exactly the card's.
    const plan = inlineAdRequestPlan({
      cardPlacementId: inlinePlacementId,
      linkPlacementId: inlineLinkPlacementId,
      policyStyle: getSessionInlineAdStyle(),
    })
    const previousEligibleCount = ctrl.eligibleSlotCounts.get(messageId) ?? 0
    if (count > previousEligibleCount) {
      ctrl.eligibleSlotCounts.set(messageId, count)
      const telemetryProperties = {
        response_id: messageId,
        chat_session_id: useChatStore.getState().chatSessionId,
        eligible_slot_count: count,
        pool_size: RESPONSE_AD_END_SLOT_COUNT,
        provider,
        surface,
        placement_id: plan.placementId,
        is_freebuff: IS_FREEBUFF,
      }
      trackInlineAdEvent(
        AnalyticsEvent.CLI_INLINE_AD_SLOT_ELIGIBLE,
        telemetryProperties,
      )
    }

    void requestLazyResponseAds({
      queue: ctrl.inlineQueue,
      messageId,
      count,
      fetchOne: async () => {
        // The dock holds an ad-network ad: a card of ours would not be drawn
        // beside it, so ask for the network only (dropped if ours comes back).
        const networkAdInView = cardRequestNeedsNetworkHint()
        const result = await fetchAd({
          placementId: plan.placementId,
          ...(networkAdInView ? { networkAdInView } : {}),
        })
        const ad = result && 'ads' in result ? (result.ads[0] ?? null) : null
        if (!ad || plan.style !== 'link') return ad
        // Drawn as the link, and its view acknowledged under the link's id.
        return {
          ...ad,
          placementId: ad.placementId ?? plan.placementId,
          inlineStyle: 'link' as const,
        }
      },
      onAd: (ad) => {
        // Only the newest answer's card is drawn: one card on screen at a
        // time, however the transcript is scrolled.
        const answerOrder = useChatStore
          .getState()
          .messages.filter(isAnswerMessage)
          .map((m) => m.id)
        const { next, superseded, dropped } = keepNewestResponseAd(
          responseAdsRef.current,
          messageId,
          ad,
          answerOrder,
        )
        // A late card for an older answer, or for one no longer in the
        // transcript: nothing drawn changes.
        if (dropped) return
        for (const old of superseded) {
          ctrl.deferredImpressions.delete(old.impUrl)
          try {
            adEngagementRegistry().noteYield(old.impUrl, 'newer_inline_card')
          } catch {
            // engagement must never break the card
          }
        }
        responseAdsRef.current = next
        setResponseAds(next)
      },
    })
  }

  requestResponseAdsRef.current = requestResponseAds

  // Don't return ads when ads should be hidden
  const visible = shouldStart && !shouldHideAds
  // Coming back with an answer held while blank: drawn from this render, so
  // the old ad never flashes before the swap the yield effect commits.
  const heldRotation = ctrlRef.current.heldRotation
  const slotAds =
    !slotYielded && yieldRef.current && heldRotation !== undefined
      ? heldRotation
      : ads
  // Rule 1: an ad-network ad held by the dock means our own cards are not
  // drawn (a sponsored offer in the dock holds no ad).
  const dockHeldAd = slotPausedRef.current ? null : (slotAds?.[0] ?? null)
  const drawableResponseAds = useMemo(
    () => suppressOurCards(responseAds, dockHeldAd),
    [responseAds, dockHeldAd?.provider, dockHeldAd?.impUrl],
  )
  drawableResponseAdsRef.current = drawableResponseAds
  return {
    // Still returned while the slot gives way: the caller keeps the slot's
    // rows (blank) so the layout does not move, and draws it again after.
    ads: visible ? slotAds : null,
    responseAds: visibleResponseAds(visible, drawableResponseAds),
    requestResponseAds,
    isLoading,
    recordClick,
    recordImpression: recordImpressionOnce,
  }
}

+ * When a drawn ad must come off the screen, on every client (web workspace and
+ * Desktop, CLI, iOS, Android).
+ *
+ * Without this an ad stays up for as long as nothing replaces it: the web
+ * banner asks for its next ad only while the person is active, and inline,
+ * phone and partner ads never rotate at all. Two limits, both counted from
+ * when this ad was first drawn (a restored ad keeps its first time):
+ *
+ * - the cap: no ad stays longer than 2 x the rotation interval, active or not;
+ * - away: once it has been shown for one rotation interval, it comes off as
+ *   soon as the person is away (idle, or the window or app hidden, unfocused
+ *   or in the background).
+ *
+ * The rotation interval is the cadence arm's `rotationMs` from
+ * `/api/v1/ads/policy` (3 min on `reduced`, 1 min on `control`); ads that do
+ * not rotate, and clients without the policy, use the `reduced` value. The
+ * policy route also sends both limits (`adMaxShownMs`, `adAwayExpiryMs`) so a
+ * client can read them as they are.
