Updates included in this release (0.2.0 since 0.1.11):
- 2026-09-30 Capped reasoning effort at high for Muse Spark, GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini: The Muse Spark default reasoning effort FREEBUFF_MUSE_SPARK_REASONING_EFFORT drops from 'xhigh' to 'high', and the GPT-6.1 Sol, Muse Spark 1.2 Contributor, and Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor rows switch their efforts arrays to EFFORTS_THROUGH_HIGH, because product capped them at high on 2026-09-29 after measuring that the xhigh rung bought ~8% more reasoning for ~26% more latency. The Gemini 3.8 Flash row fixes reasoningEffort to 'high' and uses the new EFFORTS_HIGH_ONLY ladder so the server clamps any other effort to high on every turn, per the same 2026-09-29 product decision. The GPT-6 Luna row drops 'max' from its ladder via EFFORTS_LOW_THROUGH_XHIGH while keeping its xhigh ceiling, also per the 2026-09-29 decision. The new EFFORTS_LOW_THROUGH_XHIGH and EFFORTS_HIGH_ONLY exports exist in common/src/constants/freebuff-models.ts. (touches: common/src/constants/freebuff-models.ts) [notable]
- 2026-09-30 Shared/Core update: freebuff-onboarding: Shared/Core changes across 1 file(s) (+51/−56). (touches: common/src/constants/freebuff-onboarding.ts) [minor]
